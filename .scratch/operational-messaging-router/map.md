# Build the Romania-First Operational Messaging Router

Label: wayfinder:map

## Destination

Specify, implement, verify, and launch the Romania-first Operational Messaging Router inside the Booking Product. The effort is complete when eligible customer Operational Notifications are durably delivered through the platform-owned WhatsApp Cloud API identity with SMSO.ro fallback, Merchant credit and platform pricing are operational, Merchant and Operations controls are usable, and messaging is safely available to every eligible Romanian Merchant when the Booking Product launches.

## Notes

- This effort explicitly includes implementation and verification after the decision frontier resolves. Charting this map does not itself resolve or implement any ticket.
- Parent product context: [Complete the Solo-First Merchant Appointment Scheduler](../solo-first-merchant-appointment-scheduler/map.md). That map's decision to keep WhatsApp out of scope is redrawn only for this focused effort; its remaining Booking Product decisions and invariants continue to apply.
- Preserve the existing Cloudflare-first topology, D1 authority, Effect v4 capabilities, transactional outbox, queue wake-up with cron recovery, provider-light local development, and the rule that notification delivery cannot roll back or block booking behavior.
- Settled during charting: this is an internal Booking Product capability for customer Operational Notifications, not a standalone messaging product or external messaging API.
- Settled during charting: launch covers appointment confirmation, one configurable reminder, cancellation, and reschedule. Marketing, inbound messaging, availability offers, waiting-list messages, walk-in messages, payment messages, Merchant alerts, and Provider alerts are outside launch scope.
- Settled during charting: the router attempts WhatsApp first and falls back to SMS only when WhatsApp is ineligible before submission, rejects the submission, or reports a terminal delivery failure. Delayed or unknown WhatsApp delivery does not trigger fallback.
- Settled during charting: launch uses the platform-owned WhatsApp Cloud API Business identity and shared sender plus one platform-owned SMSO.ro route. Merchant identity appears in platform-controlled message content. A second SMS gateway is deferred.
- Settled during charting: the platform chooses the lowest-cost eligible route. At launch this primarily means preferring WhatsApp before the single SMS fallback; the boundary must permit additional SMS routes later without pretending launch proves multi-gateway optimization.
- Settled during charting: platform-owned Romanian and English templates use controlled fields. Merchants cannot edit arbitrary content, choose gateways, reorder channels, or expose channel selection to customers. Only the confirmation template contains the secure Confirmation link.
- Settled during charting: messaging is opt-in per Merchant. Merchants can enable supported notification types, choose one supported reminder lead time, fund and inspect a prepaid Messaging Balance, and manually create a fresh Notification Intent from an Appointment's current state after an earlier insufficient-balance failure.
- Settled during financial modeling: the Messaging Balance charges €0.045 excluding VAT for one successfully delivered transactional Notification Intent through either WhatsApp or SMS. Provider cost and realized margin remain separate evidence; failed attempts and additional provider deliveries are platform costs, not Merchant charges.
- Settled during charting: self-service card top-ups and audited System Operator credit adjustments are launch scope. Automatic top-up is not. Insufficient balance produces a terminal Not Sent result, warns the Merchant, never blocks the domain change, and is never replayed automatically after later funding.
- Settled during prototyping: customers do not select or configure delivery channels. The existing transactional **Get appointment updates by text** step records Operational Messaging Permission for the supplied mobile number; WhatsApp-first routing and SMS fallback remain internal and are not named in that prompt. Transactional email and both marketing permissions remain separate; provider-reported or legally required suppression is honored automatically.
- Settled during charting: existing email delivery remains independent and additive. A WhatsApp or SMS failure cannot delay or cancel email.
- Settled during charting: reminders honor a platform delivery window in the Shop timezone. Immediate confirmations, cancellations, and reschedules do not wait for quiet hours.
- Settled during charting: durable at-least-once processing, semantic deduplication, provider idempotency where supported, transient retries, and explicit terminal outcomes form the reliability baseline; the product does not promise end-to-end exactly-once delivery.
- Settled during charting: Merchants see concise delivery status on the relevant Appointment timeline and Messaging Balance transactions in billing, not a separate global message-history screen. System Operators see cross-Merchant routing decisions, provider responses, provider costs, Merchant charges, and audited global channel kill switches. Routine phone-number displays are masked.
- Settled during verification planning: Operational Messaging launches with the Booking Product for every eligible Romanian Merchant; there is no post-launch allowlist or observation-volume delay. Automated verification, real-provider qualification, incident rehearsals, and a small production smoke test must pass before launch, with scoped kill switches and Merchant freezes available for containment afterward.
- Settled during execution: development uses console/fake provider adapters and deterministic contract fixtures. SMSO.ro billing, secrets, callbacks, support confirmation, and real-network qualification are deferred until a deployable adapter, callback receiver, reconciliation path, and production-like environment exist; the capped qualification must pass before the Booking Product launches, not be improvised during the final production deployment.
- Settled during execution: WhatsApp follows the same boundary. Local development and tests console-capture redacted mock template requests; Meta business verification, WABA and phone provisioning, billing, credentials, webhooks, template submission, and live qualification wait behind the implementation program and must pass before the Booking Product launches.

## Decisions so far

<!-- Resolved tickets are indexed here by name. -->

- [Research the SMSO.ro Delivery Contract](./issues/02-research-smso-ro-delivery-contract.md) — SMSO.ro fits a Workers HTTP adapter, but its undocumented idempotency, callback trust, segmentation, quota, financial, sandbox, and data-processing contract requires conservative ambiguous-send handling plus a provisioned real-route qualification gate.
- [Research Romanian Operational Messaging Obligations](./issues/03-research-romanian-operational-messaging-obligations.md) — Strictly factual booking messages can remain operational with purpose-specific GDPR bases, Meta permission and suppression controls; no mandatory channel chooser or transactional quiet-hours interval was found, while roles/transfers, closed-loop credit, tax, and retention require the documented follow-on controls and professional validation.
- [Research the WhatsApp Cloud API Delivery Contract](./issues/01-research-whatsapp-cloud-api-contract.md) — A truthful shared platform sender can use controlled Romanian/English Utility templates, but local consent and deduplication, ambiguous-submission handling, signed asynchronous delivery evidence, effective-dated pricing/error policy, and live account qualification are mandatory.
- [Implement WhatsApp Console Capture Until Production](./issues/14-implement-whatsapp-console-capture-until-production.md) — Development and test now capture deterministic, redacted Romanian appointment-confirmation requests with an independent durable channel outcome; non-local environments remain explicitly unconfigured, and live Meta work is deferred to the production-route ticket.
- [Define the Routing and Delivery Lifecycle](./issues/04-define-routing-and-delivery-lifecycle.md) — One Notification Intent owns a monotonic WhatsApp-first/SMS-fallback journey with maximum-cost reservation, scoped suppression, conservative ambiguous-send reconciliation, versioned supersession, and at most one Chargeable Delivery without an exactly-once promise.
- [Prototype Controlled Templates and Booking Disclosure](./issues/05-prototype-controlled-templates-and-disclosure.md) — Existing transactional text permission authorizes provider-agnostic mobile Operational Notifications; controlled RO/EN templates identify the Merchant, WhatsApp preserves diacritics, and every ASCII SMS fallback is bounded to one GSM-7 segment.
- [Define the Rate Card and Messaging Balance Ledger](./issues/06-define-rate-card-and-messaging-balance.md) — One delivered transactional intent costs €0.045 excluding VAT through either route, backed by exact prepaid reservations, an append-only idempotent ledger, manual top-ups, audited corrections, fiscal controls, reconciliation, and separate provider-cost margin evidence.
- [Decide the Capability, Data, and Worker Architecture](./issues/07-decide-capability-data-and-worker-architecture.md) — Notifications owns one D1-backed mobile lifecycle behind typed seams, reusing the existing Queue, Background Worker, API callback edge, and actor apps while separating email/webhooks and removing the legacy WhatsApp outbox branch.
- [Define Security, Privacy, and Provider Reconciliation](./issues/08-define-security-privacy-and-reconciliation.md) — Stage-specific privacy roles, least-authority actor boundaries, protected destinations and provider references, minimized retention, replay-safe evidence, conservative abuse limits, seven-day ambiguity closure, reconciliation, and scoped incident recovery protect customer data and Merchant balances.
- [Prototype Merchant Messaging Controls](./issues/09-prototype-merchant-messaging-controls.md) — Compact event controls configure provider-neutral messaging in Settings, while Billing owns balance and transactions, the Merchant shell owns low-balance urgency, and each Appointment owns concise status plus fresh-send recovery.
- [Prototype Operations Messaging Controls](./issues/10-prototype-operations-messaging-controls.md) — Daily work uses a masked cross-Merchant case queue with compact health signals, while privileged Containment and Finance workspaces enforce scoped permissions, previews, reasons, confirmations, and atomic audits without granting Merchant authority.
- [Define Verification and Launch Gates](./issues/11-define-verification-and-staged-release-gates.md) — Messaging launches for every eligible Romanian Merchant only after exhaustive automated, load, external-readiness, security, financial, browser, and 16-message live-provider gates pass, with measurable service objectives and scoped post-launch containment instead of a delayed rollout.
- [Synthesize and Seed the Implementation Program](./issues/12-synthesize-and-seed-implementation-program.md) — Sixteen new tracer-bullet tickets and two rewired live-route qualifications now form an acyclic path from deterministic contracts through production hardening to one conjunctive launch gate.
- [Establish the Deterministic Provider Contract Harness](./issues/16-establish-deterministic-provider-contract-harness.md) — Typed, redaction-safe provider contracts, deterministic Meta/SMSO.ro Effect doubles, fixed fixtures, and fail-closed runtime selection now form the shared verification seam for later router slices.

## Not yet specified

- Whether proven production volume, callback pressure, isolation, or reconciliation needs justify a dedicated Messaging Worker later. Launch remains inside the existing Notifications capability and Background Worker unless evidence creates a separate destination.

## Out of scope

- A standalone communications SaaS, public messaging API, or support for customers outside the Booking Product.
- Promotional or marketing campaigns, campaign analytics, attribution, arbitrary Merchant-authored content, and inferring Marketing Consent from booking behavior.
- Inbound WhatsApp or SMS conversations, reply routing, shared inboxes, chatbot behavior, and customer support threads.
- Customer-facing channel selection or messaging-preference controls unless Romanian compliance research establishes a mandatory control; provider-reported and legally required suppression remains in scope.
- Per-Merchant WhatsApp Business identities, per-Merchant SMS senders, bring-your-own-provider credentials, and direct Merchant access to upstream accounts.
- More than one SMS gateway at launch, multi-country routing, and launch markets outside Romania.
- Automatic Messaging Balance top-up, postpaid Merchant messaging, subscription-included messaging allowance, and charging Merchants for failed delivery attempts.
- A seventh deployed Messaging App or Worker at launch, a separate Merchant message-history center, and exposing raw provider payloads or unmasked phone numbers in routine UI.
- Links in reminder, cancellation, or reschedule templates; only confirmation includes the secure Confirmation link.
- Availability-offer, Waiting List, Walk-in Queue, payment, Merchant, Provider, and marketing notifications in the launch event set.
