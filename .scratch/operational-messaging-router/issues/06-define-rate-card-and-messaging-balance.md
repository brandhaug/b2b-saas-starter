# Define the Rate Card and Messaging Balance Ledger

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01, 02, 03, 04, 14

## Question

How do effective-dated platform prices, provider costs, margin, maximum-route reservation, WhatsApp and SMS billing units, successful-delivery-only Merchant charging, failed-attempt platform cost, top-ups, Operator adjustments, refunds and corrections, insufficient balance, concurrent sends, idempotency, reconciliation, taxes, invoices, low-balance notifications, and ledger retention form an auditable prepaid Messaging Balance model?

## Comments

### Resolution — 2026-07-28

The launch **Messaging Rate Card** has one simple Merchant price: **€0.045 excluding VAT for one Chargeable Delivery**, identical whether WhatsApp or SMS delivers the transactional Operational Notification. Marketing messaging remains out of scope. The Merchant price is not calculated dynamically from provider cost or SMS segments. Each Notification Intent snapshots an immutable, effective-dated Rate Card version; a future price requires at least 30 days' Merchant notice, applies only to intents created at or after its effective time, and never revalues existing EUR credit or historical charges.

Messaging Balance arithmetic uses exact thousandths of a euro, so the charge is 45 milli-euros and a €10 balance after one charge is €9.955. Provider costs retain their provider-native currency and precision separately. Meta's official calculator currently lists Romania Utility at €0.0239 in the first tier—not the €0.0175 Rest of Central & Eastern Europe rate—and SMSO.ro publicly lists low-volume SMS at €0.035, but neither mutable provider rate is a Merchant price constant. Operations receives a warning when trailing 30-day provider costs reach 80% of net Merchant charges and a critical alert at 100% or when any route's expected unit cost reaches €0.045. Pricing and routes never change automatically from those alerts.

One Merchant starts with zero balance unless a confirmed top-up or audited Operator adjustment credits it. The Owner can purchase **€10, €25, or €50** of net service credit plus applicable VAT through Stripe using the Merchant billing identity. A successful provider payment creates credit exactly once; pending or failed payment creates none. Automatic top-up is outside launch scope. Stripe unavailability disables only self-service funding: existing credit remains usable, the application remains healthy, and explicitly authorized non-cash Operator adjustments remain available. Purchased credit never expires while the Merchant is retained, remains usable for permitted existing-appointment notifications during Restricted Access, is non-transferable, and is not cash-redeemable on demand. Before permanent deletion, unused purchased credit receives an Operator refund review; non-refundable promotional credit may be removed only through an audited entry under accountant-approved terms.

Before any provider submission, one D1 transaction atomically reserves the snapshotted **€0.045** against available balance, defined as posted credits minus posted charges minus active reservations. A balance of exactly €0.045 permits one intent. Retries and WhatsApp-to-SMS fallback reuse the same reservation. The first authoritative verified delivery converts it into the intent's sole ordinary Merchant charge; a terminal outcome without delivery releases it. Acceptance, `sent`, timeout, Submission Unknown, and terminal failure never charge the Merchant. Failed attempts and additional provider deliveries remain BeeSolo Provider Messaging Costs. If authoritative evidence later invalidates a charged delivery, an idempotent compensating credit reverses the charge without deleting history.

The **Messaging Balance Ledger** is append-only. Every top-up, delivery charge, Operator adjustment, refund debit, and compensating correction is an immutable entry with exact amount, Merchant, source-scoped idempotency key, stable source identity, time, actor provenance, reason where applicable, fiscal references, and a `reverses` link for corrections. Reservations have a separate auditable lifecycle and reduce available balance without pretending to be posted charges. Unique identities are one credit per successful provider payment, one charge per Notification Intent, one provider cost per provider billing identity and unit, one debit per provider refund, one entry per submitted Operator command, and one reversal per original entry and correction reason. Repeated webhooks, workers, reconciliation, and UI commands return the existing result.

Only a System Operator with explicit financial permission can create non-cash credit or debit adjustments or perform cash refunds. Every adjustment requires a reason, reference, actor, type such as correction or goodwill, and before/after preview; a debit cannot exceed unreserved available credit. There is no self-service withdrawal or routine cancellation refund. A permitted refund is limited to a documented legal requirement, duplicate or erroneous payment, service termination, or explicit platform decision. It atomically debits available credit before requesting the provider refund and links the ledger entry, payment refund, invoice or credit note, Operator, and reason; provider failure restores credit through a compensating entry. Messaging Balance never becomes negative. Merchant Owners can view, fund, and download their records; Managers have read-only balance and usage access; Employees have no financial surface. Operator financial authority never flows through impersonation.

Available balance below €0.045 terminates the intent as **Not Sent — insufficient balance** before any provider call, immediately warns the Owner in-app and by email, never blocks the originating domain change or independent email, and is never replayed automatically after funding. A manual action may create a fresh intent from the Appointment's current state. Separately, crossing below €2 emits one Owner email and persistent in-app Low Messaging Balance Notice; it re-arms only when confirmed funding restores available balance to at least €2.

Confirmed top-ups add net service credit while applicable VAT is paid and recorded separately. The launch fiscal model issues one invoice/e-Factura per confirmed top-up, no invoice per €0.045 delivery, a downloadable balance transaction statement for consumption, and a linked credit note for a cash refund. Implementation is gated on a written Romanian accountant memo confirming advance-versus-voucher treatment, VAT timing and rounding, invoice and credit-note flow, e-Factura, promotional credit, refunds, and revenue recognition. BeeSolo's financial evidence—ledger entries, provider payment and refund references, invoices, credit notes, e-Factura acknowledgements, Operator adjustments, provider-cost evidence, and reconciliation cases—is retained for the statutory five-year accounting period calculated from 1 July following the relevant financial year, plus legal holds. Pseudonymous intent references prove charges without retaining message bodies or unmasked phone numbers for financial purposes.

Messaging Financial Reconciliation continuously ingests provider evidence and performs a daily full comparison of payment and refund facts, ledger entries, active reservations, delivery charges, provider costs, invoices, e-Factura state, and the displayed balance. Discrepancies create auditable reconciliation cases rather than silent mutation. New messaging is frozen only for a Merchant whose available balance cannot be trusted; unrelated Merchants continue. Resolution uses idempotent evidence ingestion or compensating entries.

The earlier scheduler-map rule of €0.03 per provider-accepted SMS segment is superseded for this focused Operational Messaging Router by the €0.045 per Chargeable Delivery model. The exact D1 schema, Effect services and errors, checkout interaction, Operations controls, security permissions, reconciliation schedules and recovery mechanics remain with their existing downstream architecture, security, prototype, and implementation-program tickets; no new ticket is required.

### Scope amendment — 2026-07-29

BeeSolo launches with the Owner as its only Merchant Member. Owner balance visibility, funding, records, and recovery remain requirements; Manager read-only visibility and Employee absence rules are retained only as deferred Team design and must not produce launch implementation.
