# Define Security, Privacy, and Provider Reconciliation

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01, 02, 03, 04, 06, 07

## Question

Which authorization boundaries, secret-storage rules, callback authentication and replay defenses, phone-number normalization and masking, payload-minimization rules, retention and deletion policies, provider-reference handling, audit events, rate limits, abuse controls, status monotonicity rules, reconciliation jobs, and incident procedures protect customer data and financial correctness across Merchant, Operations, WhatsApp Cloud API, and SMSO.ro boundaries?

## Comments

### Resolution — 2026-07-28

Operational Messaging uses a stage-specific **Messaging Processing Role Matrix**, not a blanket processor claim. The Merchant determines the appointment relationship and which supported Operational Notification purposes are enabled. BeeSolo independently determines platform routing, permission and suppression enforcement, security, abuse prevention, billing, reconciliation, complaints, and statutory evidence. Meta and SMSO.ro roles must be classified from their executed terms and actual processing before the Booking Product launches with Operational Messaging enabled; the DPA, privacy notice, processing records, and transfer assessment must document the allocation.

Every Merchant operation derives its Merchant from current authenticated membership and returns the ordinary not-found shape for cross-Merchant access. Owners alone enable messaging, configure notification types and reminder timing, fund Messaging Balance, and download financial records. Managers have read-only balance and usage access. Owners and Managers may create a fresh manual Notification Intent only when authorized to modify the referenced Appointment; Employees receive only masked delivery status for Appointments they may already view. Automatic notifications remain consequences of independently authorized Appointment changes. Manual sends are separately rate-limited and audited.

Operations authority is divided into explicit `messaging:read`, `messaging:control`, `messaging:finance`, `messaging:reconcile`, and `messaging:incident` permissions. Roles may bundle them, but Operations access grants none implicitly, no wildcard messaging administrator exists, and Merchant impersonation grants no Operations messaging mutation. Sensitive commands require current authoritative permission, a reason, and audit evidence. Global control, financial, reconciliation, and incident actions cannot be performed through an Impersonated Merchant Session.

Meta and SMSO.ro credentials, webhook verification material, and destination/provider encryption and fingerprint keys are platform-owned, environment- and provider-separated Cloudflare secret bindings. They never enter D1, queues, logs, traces, errors, audits, fixtures, or UI; local/test adapters cannot read production material. Only the API callback edge and Background Worker provider adapters receive the secrets they need. Rotation records a non-secret key version and uses provider-supported overlap. Missing or invalid configuration fails the route closed as `needs_configuration`; break-glass access stays outside product UI and produces infrastructure audit evidence.

Meta callbacks are verified over the raw body with the documented signature before parsing; the separate subscription challenge token is independently protected. Method, size, and shape checks fail closed. Valid duplicate and out-of-order callbacks are durably idempotent Provider Evidence and cannot repeat state, fallback, cost, or charge effects. SMSO.ro callbacks remain untrusted wake-up hints because the public contract does not authenticate them; only an authenticated provider query confirms status. Unknown or ambiguous provider references are quarantined and mutate no delivery state. Valid authenticated evidence is durably ingested before success is returned and is not discarded by generic source rate limits; forged or malformed traffic is source- and route-limited with aggregated security audit.

Each Notification Intent owns an immutable **Protected Messaging Destination**: a normalized E.164 snapshot encrypted with an environment-specific application key plus a keyed fingerprint for exact comparison, suppression, deduplication, and abuse controls. Country context must be explicit; ambiguous numbers are rejected rather than guessed. Routine surfaces show only a masked value such as `+40•••••••123`. Queue payloads, URLs, metrics, traces, reconciliation titles, audits, and ordinary logs contain no phone number. Decryption is limited to an authorized provider request or incident investigation.

Rendered WhatsApp and SMS bodies are not persisted. The router retains the template version and approved controlled facts and reconstructs content deterministically. Provider Evidence is allowlisted to provider, protected provider reference, status, provider time, normalized error class, cost facts, source, and body fingerprint. Successful raw requests and callbacks are never archived. Malformed or contradictory raw input may enter encrypted incident quarantine for at most seven days. Message text, Confirmation URLs, destinations, secrets, and full provider references are excluded from errors, audits, and observability.

Non-financial retention defaults are purpose-bound deletion deadlines: encrypted destinations remain only while actionable or reconciling and for 30 days after terminal resolution, never beyond 90 days after the last submission without a narrow incident/legal hold; controlled facts and normalized Provider Evidence remain 180 days after terminal resolution; ordinary logs remain 30 days; malformed-input quarantine remains seven days; and messaging security audits, Operator actions, incidents, and closed reconciliation cases remain two years with masked or pseudonymous identity. Financial evidence follows the already-settled Romanian five-year statutory archive without bodies or decryptable numbers. Active suppression retains only the keyed fingerprint and scope while applicable. Permanent Merchant deletion removes it unless a complaint, obligation, or narrow hold requires continued retention. Valid deletion removes eligible facts and display data while retaining only narrowly required financial, security, suppression, or dispute evidence. Holds identify exact records and purposes, not whole Merchants by default.

Raw Meta `wamid` and SMSO.ro `responseToken` values are encrypted confidential correlation keys; a keyed fingerprint supports matching, uniqueness, and deduplication. Uniqueness is scoped by environment, provider account, provider, and reference type. Provider references are never authorization, public URLs, Merchant-visible identifiers, or tenant keys. They must resolve to one existing Submission Attempt. No match creates quarantined evidence; multiple matches create a Messaging Incident and no state change. Routine Operations views use internal attempt IDs and, only where useful, a masked suffix. Full references are limited to adapters and incident-authorized access and follow Provider Evidence crypto-erasure.

Governance audits cover Merchant settings and suppression changes, fresh manual intents, kill switches, financial corrections, manual provider queries and evidence classification, reconciliation resolutions, protected-data decrypt/export, incidents, legal holds, privacy deletion, retention exceptions, crypto-erasure, sensitive authorization denials, and sustained authentication abuse. An accepted state-changing command commits its audit atomically. Each event records the real actor/auth realm, Merchant where applicable, internal identities, action, result, reason, time, correlation, and safe before/after classification—never protected payload data. Routine callbacks, retries, polls, and automatic projections remain Provider Evidence and metrics rather than duplicate audit noise.

Launch abuse defaults are ten automatic provider submission attempts per Merchant-plus-destination fingerprint in 24 hours; one fresh manual send per Appointment per five minutes and three per Appointment per 24 hours; and 20 provider submissions per Merchant per minute plus 500 per day, counting retries and fallback. Pilot Merchants may receive lower reviewed limits; increases require Operations review of expected volume, complaint history, balance funding, and account security. Provider concurrency stays below effective account limits and backs off on throttling. Limits use opaque keys, record policy version and safe denial reason, terminate before reservation, and never charge. Global and per-provider/channel kill switches plus individual Merchant freezes permit narrow containment.

Provider Evidence is append-only and route projection is deterministic, idempotent, and monotonic. Older or duplicate callbacks cannot regress state. `read` implies delivery but creates no extra charge. Conflicting terminal evidence opens a **Messaging Reconciliation Case**, freezes fallback and additional charging, and exposes a stable safe status plus `under review`. Provider event time orders observations only inside a trusted stream; arrival time, elapsed time, or silence proves nothing. Operators append classified resolution and source rather than rewriting evidence. If both routes ultimately delivered, the Merchant receives at most one ordinary charge and BeeSolo absorbs every additional provider cost.

A Submission Unknown may hold its reservation for at most seven days while reconciliation seeks authoritative evidence; no retry, fallback, or charge occurs. Operations is alerted at 24 hours. At seven days it closes as `Delivery Failed — delivery unconfirmed`, releases the reservation, and does not charge. Later verified delivery is recorded and may correct the display projection through reconciliation, but never creates a retroactive Merchant charge; BeeSolo absorbs its provider cost. Provider qualification may establish a shorter authoritative expiry, never a longer balance lock without a new policy decision.

Evidence ingestion immediately recomputes the affected intent. The existing five-minute recovery scan reclaims leases, due intents, incomplete projections, and stalled reservations. Provider-specific bounded queries reconcile ambiguous submissions where supported: SMSO.ro polling is authoritative while its callback only wakes work; Meta relies on signed webhooks and available account/billing evidence and never infers outcome from silence. A daily full comparison covers intents, reservations, charges, provider costs, payment/refund facts, invoices, and displayed balances. Daily retention processing uses retryable tombstones so partial crypto-erasure recovers safely. Reconciliation cases progress through **Open**, **Investigating**, **Resolved**, or **Waived**; waiver requires permission, reason, and audit and never rewrites evidence. All jobs use leases, cursors, and stable idempotency keys.

A **Messaging Incident** uses the narrowest effective containment: one Merchant freeze, one provider/channel kill switch, callback rejection rule, or global stop. Credential exposure disables the route, rotates and invalidates secrets, and reconciles the exposure window. Encryption-key exposure stops decryptions/submissions, rotates versions, and re-encrypts only still-required active material. Duplicate delivery freezes affected fallback/retry and never duplicates Merchant charge. Financial uncertainty freezes only Merchants whose balance is untrusted and resolves with evidence or compensating entries. Suspected personal-data exposure triggers immediate scope/risk assessment and counsel/DPO review for the applicable GDPR 72-hour supervisory-authority decision and legally required customer notice. Evidence never moves into tickets, chat, email, or ordinary logs. Global re-enable or compromised-credential recovery requires two-person approval, reconciliation, health probes, and recorded residual risk. Launch requires tabletop exercises for credential compromise, forged callbacks, ambiguity, duplicate delivery, and incorrect charging.

The exact Merchant and Operations interactions remain with their two prototype tickets. Verification thresholds and launch availability remain with the verification-and-launch-gates ticket. Exact migrations, Effect contracts, callback paths, adapter mechanics, deployment ordering, and implementation slices remain with the implementation-program synthesis; no additional ticket is required from this resolution.

### Scope amendment — 2026-07-29

BeeSolo launch Merchant authority is Owner-only. Owner settings, funding, Appointment status, and fresh-send authorization remain in scope; Manager and Employee projections or permission branches are deferred with the Team Plan. System Operator permissions and cross-Merchant Operations controls are unaffected because they belong to the separate Operations authority realm.
