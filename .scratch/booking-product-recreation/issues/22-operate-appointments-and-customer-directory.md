## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Make Appointments the Merchant operational home and expose a strictly appointment-derived Customer Directory.

**Current behavior:** Merchant operations exist only as in-memory prototype views. There is no persisted Appointment read model, membership-gated calendar/detail access, or Customer Directory derived from immutable appointment facts.

**Desired behavior:** Merchant Owners can view a Provider-oriented appointment calendar and detail pages built from immutable Appointment snapshots. The Customer Directory is a convenience view over captured Customer Details per Appointment, never a durable CRM or customer identity model.

**Key interfaces:**

- Merchant Appointment read model — supplies scheduled facts, Provider preference and assignment, immutable service and provider snapshots, Customer Details, and Pay In Person total.
- Provider-oriented calendar query — returns only authorized Merchant appointment facts with deterministic empty and seeded states.
- Customer Directory projection — derives one entry per Appointment without merging by email or phone.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] The Merchant home, calendar, and detail views show immutable Appointment facts and preserve them across catalog, Provider, and public-page changes.
- [ ] Customer entries are appointment-derived and cannot be created, edited, deduplicated, or expanded into CRM, payment, or marketing behavior.
- [ ] All Merchant reads resolve persisted membership and return non-disclosing not-found behavior across Merchant boundaries.
- [ ] Seeded and newly confirmed Appointment, calendar, detail, empty-state, snapshot, and directory tests pass.

**Out of scope:**

- Appointment mutation, cancellation, rescheduling, completion, no-show, refunds, or payment reconciliation.
- Durable Customer records, identity matching, or standalone customer management.
