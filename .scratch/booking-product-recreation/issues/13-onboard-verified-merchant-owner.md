# Onboard a verified Merchant Owner

Type: task
Category: enhancement
Status: ready-for-agent
Blocked by: 12
Parent: 10

## What to build

Let a verified person with no Merchant complete resumable Merchant Onboarding. Completion creates the Merchant authorization boundary and enough public identity to continue setup, without retaining a Workspace bridge or treating the default Provider as an operator role.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An authenticated but unverified person can only verify their email, resend verification, sign out, or recover access; they cannot create a Merchant.
- [ ] A verified person with no Merchant enters resumable Merchant Onboarding and can provide the canonical public name, reserved-safe slug, IANA timezone, and currency needed by later booking behavior.
- [ ] Completion atomically creates one Merchant, its sole Merchant Owner membership, its default Provider, and its required Unpublished Public Booking Page.
- [ ] A failed or abandoned onboarding attempt leaves a valid Better Auth user without partial Booking Product records and can be resumed safely.
- [ ] First-slice constraints enforce exactly one Owner per Merchant and at most one owned Merchant per authenticated person; no active-Merchant selector or Workspace-to-Merchant bridge exists.
- [ ] Protected Merchant App requests resolve Merchant authorization from the persisted membership on every request rather than trusting Merchant identity or role claims in the browser session.
- [ ] Merchant membership and Provider eligibility remain independent even when a solopreneur is both the Owner and default Provider.
- [ ] The typed Seed Booking Scenario builder is introduced with an explicit anchor time and deterministic IDs, and local seeding transactionally replaces only its owned Merchant graph.
- [ ] Seed and live capability adapters, real-D1 constraints, onboarding atomicity, reserved slugs, and cross-Merchant-safe authorization are covered by tests.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Let a verified person safely create their first Merchant and its minimum bookable foundation through resumable onboarding.

**Current behavior:** There is no persisted Merchant boundary, Merchant membership, Provider, Public Booking Page, or Booking seed graph. Prototype state must not become the production authorization or persistence model.

**Desired behavior:** A verified person without a Merchant can resume onboarding and atomically create exactly one Merchant, sole Owner membership, default Provider, and Unpublished Public Booking Page. Authorization resolves persisted membership on each protected request, and deterministic seed and live adapters share the same domain contract.

**Key interfaces:**

- Merchant Onboarding command — validates canonical public identity, reserved-safe slug, timezone, and currency before one atomic creation boundary.
- Merchant membership resolver — authorizes protected Merchant requests from persisted membership rather than browser role or Merchant claims.
- Seed Booking Scenario builder — supplies deterministic IDs and anchor time while replacing only its owned Merchant graph.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] Unverified people cannot create a Merchant; verified people can safely start, abandon, and resume onboarding.
- [ ] Completion is atomic, creates only the settled minimum records, and leaves no partial Booking Product state on failure.
- [ ] First-slice ownership and slug constraints hold in real D1, and membership remains independent from Provider eligibility.
- [ ] Seed/live parity, cross-Merchant non-disclosure, authorization, and onboarding rollback have automated coverage.

**Out of scope:**

- Multiple Merchant ownership, active-Merchant selection, or a Workspace bridge.
- Making the default Provider an authorization role.
