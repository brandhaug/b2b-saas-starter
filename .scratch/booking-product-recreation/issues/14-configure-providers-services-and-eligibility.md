## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Make the Merchant Catalog configurable through durable Provider, Service, lifecycle, and explicit eligibility behavior.

**Current behavior:** The production Booking Product has no end-to-end Merchant Catalog capability or persistent Merchant App workflow for configuring Services, team Providers, or their eligibility. The throwaway Merchant prototype demonstrates the intended interaction grammar with in-memory state, and Merchant Onboarding establishes the default Provider, but neither is the durable catalog behavior this slice requires.

**Desired behavior:** An authorized Merchant Owner can create and edit merchant-scoped Services and, for Team presentation, Providers. Services carry validated display data, positive money and duration, currency, and an Active or Inactive lifecycle. Providers carry a display name, default designation, and the same lifecycle. Provider-Service eligibility is an explicit persisted association. Solo presentation continues to use the persisted default Provider while hiding unnecessary Provider administration. Inactive records remain readable for historical use but cannot enter new Booking Sessions. Service editing retains the reduced Details-to-Providers flow, and Team Provider editing retains the Profile, Services, and Schedule vocabulary without importing Legacy Source architecture.

**Key interfaces:**

- Merchant Catalog capability — lists and mutates Services, Providers, and eligibility within the resolved Merchant boundary, with typed validation and authorization-safe failures and equivalent test and live adapters.
- Service contract — exposes name, optional display description and category, positive integer duration, positive integer minor-unit price, currency, and Active or Inactive status.
- Provider contract — exposes display name, default designation, Active or Inactive status, and merchant-scoped Service eligibility.
- Provider-Service eligibility association — persists each eligible pair explicitly and rejects cross-Merchant or nonexistent references; eligibility is never derived from membership, default designation, or embedded ID arrays.
- Merchant App catalog workflows — provide the reduced Service Details-to-Providers flow and Team-only Provider administration while keeping Solo setup uncluttered.
- Seed Booking Scenario — remains the single authored fixture and expands to two Providers and three Services with intentionally different eligibility; Solo and incomplete cases are derived variants.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [x] Merchant Owners can create and edit validated Services and Team Providers, and all reads and mutations are scoped to the resolved Merchant without cross-Merchant disclosure or mutation.
- [x] Provider-Service eligibility is stored as explicit merchant-consistent associations and cannot be inferred from Merchant membership, default Provider status, or denormalized ID arrays.
- [x] Active records are available to new Booking Sessions; changing a record to Inactive preserves it for history while excluding it from new booking and readiness inputs, with no hard-delete workflow.
- [x] Solo presentation hides Provider administration but continues to configure Services against the persisted default Provider; Team presentation exposes the reduced Profile, Services, and Schedule vocabulary.
- [x] Service editing follows a Details-to-Providers interaction, validates positive duration and price plus currency, and retains optional description and category behavior.
- [x] The canonical fixture, its derived variants, test adapters, live adapters, real D1 constraints, and Merchant App workflows have automated coverage for lifecycle, validation, eligibility, seed parity, and tenant isolation.

**Out of scope:**

- Recurring Schedule Rule persistence or editing, Availability derivation, Booking Readiness publication, and the public booking page behavior owned by the next slice.
- Brand, Shop, location assignment, tax, kiosk, prepayment, or per-Provider overrides for Service price, duration, visibility, or payment behavior.
- Provider login identity, Merchant membership or permissions, private employment/contact data, notifications, payroll, appointment history, one-off schedule exceptions, and hard deletion.
