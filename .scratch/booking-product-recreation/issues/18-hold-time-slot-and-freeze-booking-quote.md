## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Derive bookable Availability and atomically hold one Time Slot with an immutable Booking Quote.

**Current behavior:**
The Booking App renders a source-faithful scheduling prototype with a fixed calendar strip, three fixture times, selected-time feedback, and a simulated slot-lost state. Time selection exists only in React memory. The Booking capability and D1 model do not yet derive Availability from Merchant scheduling data, exclude overlapping Appointments or competing holds, assign a concrete Provider for Any Provider, create an exclusive Time Slot Hold, or freeze accepted Service and Provider facts in a Booking Quote.

**Desired behavior:**
An active, capability-authorized Booking Session with valid Provider Preference and ordered Service selections should receive derived Availability. Candidate Time Slots must be computed from recurring Provider Schedule Rules, total Service duration, Provider-Service eligibility, confirmed Appointment conflicts, and unexpired Time Slot Holds; generated Time Slots must never become persisted records.

Selecting a candidate must atomically revalidate it and create one exclusive ten-minute Time Slot Hold for the exact Provider interval. A Specific Provider selection retains that Provider. An Any Provider selection must choose one concrete eligible Provider within the same atomic operation while preserving Any Provider as the customer's original preference. Competing attempts for an overlapping Provider interval must have at most one winner.

The winning hold must carry an immutable Booking Quote containing the scheduled interval, assigned Provider facts, original Provider Preference, and ordered Primary and Additional Service snapshots with roles, durations, positive prices, one currency, and an exact summed total. Later Merchant edits, Service or Provider lifecycle changes, Schedule Rule changes, or Public Booking Page unpublishing must not rewrite a valid hold or quote. Reads and Booking Session activity must not extend the fixed hold deadline. After expiry, the interval is available again and the Session must acquire a new hold and quote from current catalog facts.

### BeeSolo scheduling amendment — 2026-07-30

The immutable-quote rule does not let a provisional Time Slot Hold prevent the Owner from responding to a scheduling change. Except for a Shop-timezone change, which waits until no hold is active, a BeeSolo mutation to Schedule Rules, Booking Window controls, Service duration, or Service Buffers takes effect immediately and atomically invalidates only the active holds it makes unavailable. The invalidated hold and quote end rather than being rewritten; unaffected holds remain valid and immutable, and the affected Booking Party retains its other selections for slot-lost recovery. Confirmed Appointment snapshots remain immutable. This targeted amendment supersedes only the earlier requirement that these later scheduling changes could not affect a valid hold.

Lost, expired, invalid, and concurrently claimed Time Slots should return non-disclosing recovery states without corrupting the still-valid Provider Preference and Service selections. The Booking App should preserve its established calendar strip, time density, three-column grid, selected-time feedback, and explicit slot-lost message while replacing fixture selection with server-authoritative Availability and holds.

**Key interfaces:**

- Availability query — accepts the authorized Booking Session context and a bounded date range, then returns derived candidate Time Slots without persisting generated slots or exposing private Provider scheduling data.
- Time Slot Hold command — atomically revalidates a chosen candidate, resolves Any Provider when applicable, prevents overlapping holds and confirmed Appointments for the assigned Provider, and replaces or records the Session's one current hold.
- `TimeSlotHold` — represents the assigned Provider interval, creation time, fixed ten-minute expiry, and accepted Booking Quote owned by one active Booking Session.
- `BookingQuote` — preserves the scheduled interval, assigned Provider snapshot, original Provider Preference, ordered Service roles and snapshots, durations, positive minor-unit prices, currency, and exact total for the lifetime of the hold.
- Scheduling clock — is injectable for deterministic Availability and expiry behavior; read and Session-activity paths cannot mutate the hold deadline.
- Booking App scheduling states — render derived days and times, selected hold feedback, no-times behavior, and safe recovery from an expired or concurrently lost slot.
- Seed Booking Scenario — uses one explicit anchor time and includes past history plus a future Appointment whose interval deterministically blocks Availability.

**Acceptance criteria:**
