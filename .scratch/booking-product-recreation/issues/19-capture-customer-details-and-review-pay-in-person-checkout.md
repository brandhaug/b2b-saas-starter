## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Persist minimum unverified Customer Details and present a server-authoritative Pay In Person checkout review.

**Current behavior:**
The Booking App has a fixture-backed customer-details and checkout prototype whose state lives only in React memory. It does not authorize or persist Customer Details through a Booking Session, read an accepted Booking Quote from D1, or protect checkout facts from browser substitution. The prototype also exposes a Pay Now selector and payment-state copy that the settled first slice explicitly excludes.

**Desired behavior:**
An active, capability-authorized Booking Session with a valid Time Slot Hold accepts a required customer name and email plus a nullable phone as unverified Customer Details. Those values belong only to that booking attempt: matching contact values must never identify a returning customer, prefill private data, reveal another Appointment, or create a durable Customer identity.

Checkout is a stable review boundary over server-owned Session state. It displays the accepted time, concrete assigned Provider, ordered Service snapshots, exact total and currency from the held Booking Quote, and the fixed read-only Pay In Person Checkout Path. The browser may submit Customer Details and invoke the `Book` action, but it cannot provide or override the Checkout Path, quote, prices, Service snapshots, Provider assignment, or other accepted booking facts.

The customer journey uses `Go to checkout` to enter a screen headed `Confirm booking`, with `Book` as the final action. Beneath that action, a non-checkbox disclosure links to the Public Site's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. No consent record or policy-version snapshot is created. Expired Sessions restart safely; expired or lost holds return the customer to scheduling without presenting stale quote facts; invalid Customer Details remain correctable without corrupting the Session or accepted quote.

**Key interfaces:**

- `CustomerDetails` — validates and represents required name and email plus nullable phone as unverified booking contact data, not customer identity.
- Booking Session customer-details mutation — requires the matching Session capability and active lifecycle, persists only accepted Customer Details, and returns typed, non-disclosing validation or lifecycle failures.
- Checkout review read model — derives customer-visible time, assigned Provider, ordered Service snapshots, total, currency, and fixed Checkout Path exclusively from the authorized Session and its current hold and quote.
- Booking confirmation command contract — accepts no client-authored Checkout Path, quote, price, Service snapshot, Provider assignment, or other authoritative booking fact.
- Booking App checkout states — render successful review, field validation, expired Session, expired hold, and lost-slot recovery with the settled customer-facing copy and policy links.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [x] Only an active, capability-authorized Booking Session with a valid hold can persist required name and valid email plus nullable phone as Customer Details; invalid input produces correctable field feedback without partial or cross-Session mutation.
- [x] Repeated email or phone values never link Appointments, prefill or disclose private data, authorize access, create an account, or create or merge a durable Customer record.
- [x] Checkout renders the accepted time, concrete Provider, ordered Service facts, exact total, currency, and read-only Pay In Person Checkout Path from the current server-owned hold and Booking Quote.
- [x] Browser inputs and confirmation commands cannot replace or inject Checkout Path, price, currency, Service snapshots, Provider assignment, time, or any other accepted quote fact.
- [x] The journey uses `Go to checkout`, `Confirm booking`, and `Book` exactly, and includes non-checkbox Terms of Service and Privacy Policy links to the Public Site without persisting consent or policy versions.
- [x] Checkout renders no payment selector, credential, status, tax, tip, fee, discount, deposit, cancellation-fee, or no-show-charge behavior or copy.
- [x] A valid capability for an expired Session receives the settled safe restart response; an expired or lost hold clears stale review facts and returns to scheduling while preserving only still-valid Session selections.
- [x] Capability, live-D1, route, and component tests cover successful review, invalid Customer Details, repeated contact values, quote-tampering attempts, expired Session, expired hold, lost slot, and the absence of deferred payment behavior.

**Out of scope:**

- Atomically creating the Appointment, issuing Confirmation access, reducing the Session, or appending and publishing durable outbox work.
- Customer accounts, contact verification, identity matching, saved profiles, CRM behavior, marketing consent, or policy-version tracking.
- Pay Now, payment providers, payment credentials or status, taxes, tips, fees, discounts, deposits, refunds, cancellation fees, or no-show charges.
