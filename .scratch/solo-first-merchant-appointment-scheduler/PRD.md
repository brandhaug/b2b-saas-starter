# Complete the beesolo Merchant Appointment Scheduler

Status: ready-for-agent

## Problem Statement

The existing Booking Product proves important architectural and booking invariants, but it does not yet operate as a complete production appointment scheduler for a solopreneur barber or salon professional. A Merchant Owner needs one coherent Solo product through which they can start a trial, configure one Shop, publish a customer booking experience, manage daily appointments and demand, communicate transactionally, understand operational results, meet privacy obligations, recover from provider failures, and safely maintain access through the Merchant Subscription lifecycle.

Without a single implementation contract, delivery agents would have to invent behavior across onboarding, scheduling, Appointment operations, Appointment Series, the Customer Directory, Walk-in Queue, Waiting List, Notifications, reporting, privacy, billing, the Public Site, operations, migration, rollout, and rollback. That would risk contradictions with the existing conflict-safe booking transaction, immutable Appointment snapshots, Merchant isolation, Effect capability boundaries, D1 authority, and durable outbox recovery.

The launch boundary also needs to remain unambiguously Solo. The Owner is the Merchant's only Merchant Member and sole active Provider for one Shop. Team plans, additional Members or Providers, invitations, roles, per-seat billing, Provider selection, and Team-oriented schedules must not leak back into product behavior, navigation, pricing, data writes, fixtures, or release evidence.

## Solution

Deliver **beesolo**, always styled in lowercase, as a Cloudflare-first, Solo-only appointment scheduler for one Merchant Owner operating one Shop as its sole active Owner-Provider.

The product will provide a bilingual Public Site, a Merchant App, a customer Booking App reached only through the Public Site, a staff-only Operations App, shared Effect v4 capabilities, authoritative D1 persistence, and durable Background Worker processing. Customers book as guests using capability-protected Booking Sessions and Confirmations. The Merchant owns a conservative Merchant-scoped Customer Directory while every Appointment preserves immutable Customer, Service, Provider, duration, buffer, and price snapshots.

The Owner will receive a fourteen-day Solo trial and a resumable activation checklist covering Business Details, professional profile confirmation, Services, Schedule Rules, Booking Policies, Transactional Email readiness, a side-effect-free Launch Test, and first publication. After launch, the day-centric Merchant schedule, explicit Appointment commands, finite weekly Appointment Series, Walk-in Queue, Waiting List, reporting, External Collections, customer privacy operations, and Solo billing recovery form one operational system.

Transactional Email is required for customer demand and covers Appointment, Waiting List, and Walk-in lifecycles. Optional Appointment-only mobile delivery uses the separately governed WhatsApp-first/SMS-fallback Operational Messaging Router, its Messaging Balance, and its effective-dated €0.045-per-verified-Chargeable-Delivery Rate Card. Provider outages never undo committed domain work; transactional outbox state and scheduled recovery make delivery durable and observable.

Production promotion will be controlled by a non-waivable Core Production Gate and independent Feature Activation Gates for optional provider-backed capabilities. One immutable Release Readiness Record will bind the exact candidate, schema, configuration, legal approval, migration and restore evidence, accessibility evidence, parity evidence, dashboards, alerts, runbooks, and activated provider routes.

## Implementation Decisions

- The launch product is Solo-only. One Merchant has one Shop, exactly one Merchant Member who is the Owner, and one active Owner-Provider automatically eligible for every Service. Lower-level historical Provider identity may remain only where required to interpret existing snapshots and migrations; it must not create additional-provider product behavior.
- Keep Public Site, Merchant App, Booking App, Operations App, API plumbing, and Background Worker as distinct Cloudflare Worker surfaces. The Public Site remains the only production customer ingress and relays Merchant-scoped Booking traffic through a service binding. The API surface publishes no Platform API business contract at launch.
- Keep D1 authoritative for product, entitlement, access, outbox, notification, privacy, audit, and operational facts. Runtime applications use Live D1 adapters; deterministic Seed adapters remain test-only and never become a production fallback.
- Put business behavior in bounded-context Effect v4 capabilities. Route handlers, React components, provider adapters, Queue consumers, and scheduled triggers adapt those contracts and do not duplicate policy.
- Preserve anonymous capability-protected Booking Sessions, conflict-safe Time Slot Holds, exact-purpose Confirmation access, conservative Customer Record matching, immutable Appointment snapshots, idempotency, optimistic concurrency, Merchant isolation, and transactional outbox recovery.
- Keep Availability derived rather than persisted. Derive it from civil-time Weekly Working Hours, replacing Date Overrides, exact Blocked Time, Start-Time Interval, Service duration and buffers, Booking Window, active Service facts, subscription and publication gates, non-terminal Appointment conflicts, and unexpired holds.
- Model Schedule Changes as auditable current configuration changes, not reconstructable versions. Existing Appointment and quote snapshots preserve history. Schedule-impact previews and derived Schedule Conflicts protect commitments.
- Model Appointment changes as explicit revisioned commands instead of a generic patch. Commands record server time, actor and impersonation provenance, idempotency, before-and-after facts, reasons, notification choice, and operation identity.
- Materialize Appointment Series atomically as independent Appointments under one durable grouping. After creation, mutations are Appointment-scoped except the dedicated command that cancels every remaining Scheduled member.
- Persist Merchant-scoped Customer Records separately from immutable Appointment Customer Details. Exact normalized destinations may identify only an unambiguous record; names never match alone. Ordinary merge and split are Owner-controlled, while privacy processing has only the narrower permissioned Privacy Identity Partition.
- Treat External Collections as append-only, unverified off-platform facts. They never become a Payment, Payment Attempt, verified receipt, automatic charge, platform refund, or revenue figure.
- Implement Walk-in Queue and Waiting List as distinct stateful capabilities with protected customer access, atomic revisions, idempotency, histories, explicit Owner and Worker authority, and conflict-safe Appointment conversion.
- Keep Waiting List offers email-only at launch. Keep mobile routing Appointment-only. A Waiting List offer creates no Appointment or hold until the customer accepts and current facts are atomically revalidated.
- Transactional Email is mandatory for Merchant Activation and new public demand. Store domain transitions and Notification Operations or outbox work atomically; deliver asynchronously; recover through Queue wake-ups and scheduled sweeps; never roll back domain truth because of delivery failure.
- Reuse the Operational Messaging Router as the only mobile boundary. Snapshot the effective-dated €0.045 Rate Card, reserve one possible charge, charge only one verified Chargeable Delivery, and treat SMS segment counts as provider-cost evidence. Automatic Messaging Balance top-up is outside launch.
- Keep mobile activation behind the authoritative Messaging Launch Gate, including written route terms, live qualification, controlled one-segment GSM-7 templates, and expected blended Provider Messaging Cost at or below €0.036 per Chargeable Delivery. Reaching the €0.045 charge level blocks launch or further rollout/configuration changes under the router incident rules.
- Keep the Merchant App day-centric across responsive layouts. Use the searchable Settings index as the persistent control plane; use readiness-led guidance before publication and only conditional needs-attention alerts after launch. Keep operational actions in Appointment, Walk-in, Waiting List, reporting, and billing contexts.
- Name the Solo profile surface “Your professional profile.” Expose no Team plan choice, invitations, seats, roles, additional Providers, Provider selector, Provider filters, Team schedule layout, upgrade, downgrade, or ownership-transfer path.
- Keep reporting server-derived from authoritative facts on demand. Use one consistent generated-at read, Shop-local period attribution, explicit metric definitions, privacy-minimal drill-downs, and one CSV per primary fact. Generated exports are encrypted and expire after twenty-four hours.
- Implement Privacy Requests under standing Merchant instructions with accountless intake, exact-destination verification, risk review, immutable preflight, separate read/review/erase permissions, recently two-factor-authenticated Operator Sessions, idempotent correction and erasure plans, provider obligations, fixed retention, and value-minimized audit.
- Keep the Privacy Action Ledger outside the primary Merchant-data restore boundary. A restored environment remains in maintenance until privacy replay, artifact invalidation, subscription and provider reconciliation, and safe Queue recovery pass.
- Make the authoritative D1 Merchant Subscription projection the source of product access. Stripe events and API reads are provider evidence. Trial, renewal, Grace, restriction, cancellation, chargeback, refund consequence, retention, and recovery transitions are explicit and reconciled.
- Publish only bilingual product, Blog, Help, FAQ, legal, privacy-intake, and Merchant booking content. Remove starter/developer content and public API promises. Retired docs and changelog routes return `410 Gone`. Indexing follows effective Merchant availability and excludes sessions, continuations, checkout, Confirmation, unavailable, preview, and degraded surfaces.
- Migrate active product, package, asset, manifest, metadata, and deployable identity to lowercase beesolo through compatibility-aware cutovers. Persisted and deployed identifiers must never be destructively recreated merely for renaming.
- Keep the `.ics` export generated on demand from an authorized current Confirmation. It is a privacy-minimal snapshot, not a subscription, persisted artifact, provider-specific link, synchronization channel, or accepted external mutation.
- Use forward-only expand-and-contract migrations. Every migration needs empty-database and supported-production-schema proof, a no-change-on-failed-preflight guarantee, bounded resumable backfill where needed, an application compatibility window, pre/post invariant evidence, and a reviewed forward compensation path.
- Application rollback routes traffic to a compatible previous Worker. Schema rollback is forbidden. Point-in-time restore is last resort and must be rehearsed with privacy replay and provider/Queue reconciliation before traffic returns.
- Treat every externally reachable route, callback, Queue consumer, and scheduled trigger as a Production Ingress with one owner and contract evidence. Use named browser Release Journeys to cover complete cross-surface behavior and machine contracts for callbacks, queues, scheduled work, and remaining routes.
- Maintain seven operational dashboard families: Release Health; Booking Integrity; Authorization and Isolation; Solo Subscription; Notifications and Queues; Privacy Operations; and Migration and Data Recovery. Use safe identifiers and masked or aggregate data.
- Establish non-waivable Core Production Gate evidence for exact candidate identity, authorization, Merchant isolation, booking integrity, concurrency, idempotency, migrations, rollback, restore, privacy, accessibility, security, capacity, observability, and runbooks. Use independent Feature Activation Gates for optional mobile providers.
- Require a Release Readiness Record binding commit and artifact digests, schema, restore point, configuration fingerprints, gate outcomes, Feature Activation states, manual attestations, provider qualification, legal approval, parity revision, dashboard/alert routing, runbook exercises, approver, and promotion time.

  3. Complete shared authority, idempotency, optimistic-concurrency, history, audit, outbox, and real-D1 capability test harnesses.
  4. Complete the Solo Merchant Subscription projection, Stripe event ingestion and reconciliation, trial/grace/restriction/recovery behavior, and Billing surface.
  5. Complete Merchant Activation, one-Shop Business Details, professional profile, Services, Schedule Rules, Booking Policies, Notification Readiness, Preview Mode, Launch Test, and publication.
  6. Complete public Merchant discovery and the guest Booking journey through contention-safe confirmation, protected Confirmation operations, and `.ics` export.
  7. Complete the responsive day ledger, Merchant Appointment commands, Appointment History, External Collections, and Appointment Series.

## Testing Decisions

- A good test asserts externally visible behavior: authorized results, non-disclosing failures, state transitions, durable history, persisted outbox consequences, retries, reports, exports, redirects, rendered states, or recovery outcomes. It does not couple to private Effect composition, query shape, React component structure, or incidental provider SDK behavior.
- Use one primary application seam: typed Effect capability and HTTP behavior exercised against Live D1. This is the highest reusable boundary at which Merchant scope, authorization, validation, idempotency, revision conflicts, atomic writes, typed errors, audit, outbox effects, and recovery can be proven together.
- Use capability contract suites to prove that Live D1 and deterministic test adapters satisfy the same service shape where a test adapter remains appropriate. Runtime applications must always select Live D1 and return `CapabilityUnavailable` or a more specific typed state when dependencies fail.
- Use real-D1 integration tests for schema invariants, transaction boundaries, concurrency, idempotency, optimistic revisions, unique active states, conservative Customer Record matching, no-overlap scheduling, outbox atomicity, subscription convergence, reporting consistency, privacy preflight invalidation, and restore replay.
- Use focused browser Release Journeys rather than one browser test per page. Cover the fifteen mandatory families: published-Shop discovery; Solo start and publication; last-slot contention; customer Appointment management; daily ledger operation; Appointment Series; safe Availability changes; Walk-in Queue; Waiting List fulfillment; Customer Record safety; subscription restriction and recovery; truthful reporting and export; Privacy Request processing; support impersonation; and safe required-dependency degradation.
- Give each Release Journey happy, negative, responsive, Romanian/English, and accessibility variants. No critical variant may silently skip because a local provider or fixture is absent; controlled adapters or explicit Feature Activation evidence must cover the intended boundary.
- Preserve existing route-ownership and Public Site service-binding tests to prove that only the Public Site is production booking ingress and that private Booking App routes, Merchant credentials, and customer capabilities do not cross surfaces.
- Add a generated authorization and Merchant-isolation matrix for every Merchant-scoped read, mutation, search, bulk operation, export, capability, queued action, and callback correlation. Prove no-session denial, same-shape cross-Merchant not-found, access-hold denial, Restricted Access exceptions, valid Owner scope, and attributed impersonation.
- Every denied mutation must prove zero domain change, outbox work, notification, audit transition that implies success, and financial consequence. Security telemetry may record the attempt without revealing another Merchant or Customer.
- Exercise production-shaped races: 25 contenders for one Time Slot; hold expiry versus confirmation; customer versus Owner booking; reschedule versus cancellation; stale revision versus current command; series all-or-nothing creation; External Collection retry; overlapping worker sweeps; Waiting List offer allocation and public booking; walk-in start and Appointment creation; privacy approval versus changed data; and duplicate or out-of-order provider evidence.
- For every idempotent command, prove same-key/same-payload replay returns the original result, same-key/different-payload rejects, and neither path duplicates Appointments, Customer links, histories, External Collections, notifications, charges, audit events, sessions, holds, or outbox items.
- Add provider-adapter contract tests with controlled fakes for Transactional Email, Stripe, WhatsApp, SMS, Turnstile, Queue, callbacks, and observability. Verify signature, replay, timeout, duplicate, contradictory, out-of-order, unknown-submission, invalid-destination, insufficient-balance, reservation-release, and terminal-failure behavior.
- Keep provider qualification separate from adapter correctness. Email and billing configuration must pass the Core Production Gate. WhatsApp/SMS require written terms, capped live qualification, controlled-template proof, cost evidence, callbacks, fallback, reconciliation, and kill-switch exercises before their Feature Activation Gate opens.
- Add Background Worker fault-injection tests for pre-commit failure, commit-before-enqueue failure, commit-before-ack redelivery, poison messages, dead-letter redrive, stuck claims, overdue reminders, duplicate callbacks, scheduled sweep overlap, and recovery of 1,000 committed outbox items without semantic duplication.
- Add migration tests from an empty database and the immediately supported production schema using production-shaped fixtures. Verify preflight abort leaves all rows unchanged, expand/candidate/previous-Worker compatibility, bounded resumable backfills, Solo invariant checks, pre/post counts, contract timing, and compensating forward repair.
- Rehearse application rollback and point-in-time restore on disposable production-shaped data. Prove Privacy Action Ledger replay, erased/corrected/quarantined/held data protection, artifact invalidation, subscription reconciliation, provider reconciliation, and safe Queue drain or redrive before maintenance ends.
- Use the existing Storybook-style state seam only for isolated visual and accessibility states, not as evidence for domain correctness. Use it to cover loading, saving, conflict, empty, unavailable, needs-attention, Restricted Access, repeated-time, long-content, mobile-sheet, and high-contrast states.
- Add automated accessibility scanning to every journey state and manual keyboard-only, 200% zoom, 400% reflow, reduced-motion, high-contrast, VoiceOver/Safari, and NVDA/Firefox evidence for representative desktop and mobile journeys. WCAG 2.2 Level A or AA failures in launch-critical journeys block release.
- Add content and indexing tests for bilingual route completeness, MDX frontmatter, broken links, lowercase beesolo identity, legal placeholders, forbidden starter and Team vocabulary, Pricing-only Team teaser, retired `410` routes, canonical URLs, `hreflang`, `x-default`, sitemaps, noindex rules, merchant effective availability, manifests, assets, and unsupported claims.
- Add reporting contract tests for Shop Reporting Timezone and DST, service/activity period attribution, mutually exclusive status counts, matured denominators, utilization, Walk-in and Waiting List cohorts, notification outcome separation, External Collection labels, consistent reads, privacy-minimal CSVs, header-only exports, retention cleanup, and explicit empty/error/pending states.
- Add privacy state-machine tests for generic intake responses, rate limits, exact-destination proof, enhanced evidence, claims, permissions, recent two-factor authentication, preflight invalidation, identity partition, package delivery, verified correction, erasure quarantine and retries, holds, withdrawal after partial execution, audit minimization, notices, deadlines, cleanup, and restore replay.
- Add subscription tests for trial creation uniqueness, expiry, Grace timing, cancellation, chargeback, refund consequences, retention warnings, restriction permissions, recovery, duplicate/delayed/contradictory events, provider outage, API reconciliation, and the invariant that provider status never grants authority directly.
- Keep the parity ledger synchronized with behavior changes. Its structural check must report no unowned, duplicate, orphaned, planned, placeholder, or skipped launch state. Add explicit regression assertions for the absence of Team navigation, Team pricing, additional Providers, Provider choice, invitations, roles, seats, Platform API controls, external calendar synchronization, and retired starter surfaces.
- Verify the candidate against the defined performance and recovery targets: reads p95 below 500 ms and p99 below 1.5 seconds, Availability p95 below 750 ms, D1 mutation p95 below one second, booking confirmation p95 below two seconds excluding asynchronous delivery, unexpected errors below 1%, 100 concurrent Booking Sessions across Merchants for fifteen minutes, and investigation of any regression above 20% from the previous accepted candidate.
- Require staging exercises for every alert route and owned runbook. No test or runbook may rely on undocumented direct production-row editing or manual recreation of customer Notifications.
- Existing prior art includes per-capability Effect service tests, Live D1 migration and integration suites, Public Site route-ownership tests, focused Playwright journeys, auth/session integration tests, PWA network-fresh regressions, provider callback tests, and parity-ledger structural checks. Extend these seams instead of creating a parallel framework.

## Out of Scope

- Team Plan implementation, multiple Merchant Members, invitations, Manager or Employee roles, per-seat billing, ownership transfer, Team upgrade or downgrade, additional Providers, editable Provider eligibility, Provider selection, Team navigation, and week-board schedule layouts.
- Multiple Shops, Brands as a Merchant-managed product surface, franchises, cross-location schedules, and cross-location reporting.
- Online customer payments, deposits, saved cards, connected accounts, payouts, disputes, platform refunds, verified receipts, and automatic no-show or cancellation charges.
- Resources, chairs, rooms, equipment, classes, group capacity, attendee enrollment, packages, memberships, gift cards, loyalty, referrals, commissions, payroll, and advanced tax or finance reconciliation.
- Mandatory Customer Accounts, customer login or dashboard, cross-Merchant identity, saved customer payment methods, general intake-form building, file uploads, medical forms, and e-signatures.
- Google, Microsoft, Apple, or other external-calendar synchronization; inbound calendar changes; calendar subscriptions; provider-specific add-to-calendar links; and Merchant calendar integrations.
- Platform API business routes, API Tokens, Webhook Endpoints, external event catalogs, Webhook Delivery Attempts, replay, signing, secret rotation, developer credentials, OpenAPI/Scalar publication, and customer or Merchant booking-write APIs.
- Two-way or inbound WhatsApp/SMS, customer chat, arbitrary message templates, Merchant-selectable routing, manual sends or resends, marketing campaigns, attribution automation, promotional messaging, and mobile Waiting List or Walk-in messaging.
- Automatic Messaging Balance top-up and a separate SMS-specific balance, Merchant price, or charging lifecycle.
- Full month Appointment grids, persistent desktop week boards, offline mutations, cached Availability, background sync, push notifications, and new realtime transport without a separately proven coordination need.
- Persisted Availability, complete reconstruction of previously displayed Time Slots, recurring Blocked Time, overnight working intervals, endless or daily/monthly/custom Appointment recurrence, and bulk historical Appointment import.
- Public testimonials, ratings, customer-count, uptime, superiority, unverified performance, or provider-logo claims; a public CMS; site-wide command-palette search; and external developer documentation.
- Government-ID, selfie, or payment-data verification for Privacy Requests; Merchant approval of individual privacy cases; general operator-driven Customer deduplication; and a second-operator privacy approval requirement at launch.
- Destructive schema rollback, undocumented production database editing, or treating screenshots, structural parity, provider claims, or manual judgment as substitutes for release evidence.

## Further Notes

- The resolved Team membership and pricing design is historical future design only. It must not generate launch implementation, UI, fixtures, migration allowances, or active documentation.
- The Operational Messaging Router map is authoritative for provider-neutral mobile routing, Messaging Balance, Rate Card, templates, callbacks, qualification, financial reconciliation, and the Messaging Launch Gate. This specification intentionally does not fork those rules.
- The earlier €0.03-per-provider-accepted-SMS-segment rule and SMS Balance language are superseded. SMS segments remain provider-cost evidence only.
- The current architecture documentation may still describe accepted full-parity branches or Workers that are operationally present but deferred as product promises. Launch behavior and public documentation must follow this PRD's narrower beesolo boundary.
- Production legal launch requires reviewed Privacy and Terms content, the operating legal entity and contacts, the Data Processing Agreement, controller contact details, retention schedule, privacy-request language, billing and cancellation facts, and provider disclosures. Placeholders fail the Core Production Gate.
- Core production may launch with mobile messaging disabled only if every mobile surface clearly represents the capability as unavailable and Transactional Email remains fully ready. A required dependency may never be represented as successful when unconfigured.
- Release evidence older than seven days must be rerun before first production promotion, and any material code, artifact, schema, configuration, template, or policy change invalidates affected evidence.
