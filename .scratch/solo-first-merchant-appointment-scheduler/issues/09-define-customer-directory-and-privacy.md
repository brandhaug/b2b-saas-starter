# Define the Customer Directory and Privacy Contract

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

How should anonymous Customer Details become a merchant-scoped Customer Directory with deduplication or merge rules, appointment history, merchant notes, the optional booking note, contact preferences and consent evidence, search, import/export, deletion, retention, and ban behavior without creating mandatory Customer Accounts or rewriting immutable Appointment snapshots?

## Comments

### Resolution — 2026-07-30

BeeSolo replaces the previously appointment-derived directory with durable **Customer Records** inside each Merchant boundary. A Customer Record is reusable Merchant-owned directory identity, not a verified Customer Account, login, or cross-Merchant profile. Public confirmation, Merchant-Created Appointment, and Record Completed Appointment conservatively match or create the Customer Record, link it to the new Appointment, and append an observation in the same transaction as Appointment creation. Abandoned Booking Sessions, Preview Mode, quotes, and Time Slot Holds create no directory data. Customer Directory Import is the sole record-creation path without an Appointment.

Every Appointment continues to preserve the Customer Details accepted for that Appointment as immutable revisioned snapshots. The linked Customer Record owns current reusable facts and observations but is not a mutable master copy of Appointment history. Editing preferred details, matching records, merging or splitting them, archiving them, or changing notes and preferences never silently rewrites those snapshots. Customer Data Erasure is the sole exceptional privacy mechanism allowed to anonymize historical personal values, and it preserves non-personal Appointment facts and audit evidence that erasure occurred.

Automatic attachment uses normalized email or phone only within one Merchant. It succeeds only when the supplied contact identifies exactly one Customer Record and every other supplied identifier is absent or non-conflicting; a name alone never matches. Ambiguous, shared, or conflicting details create a separate possible duplicate. An unambiguous new Appointment observation may add a new non-conflicting contact point and suggest a recently observed name, but it never silently replaces Owner-selected preferred name, email, or phone. Historical contact points may locate a record but are labelled and cannot become a communication destination automatically.

Only the Owner may confirm a **Customer Record Merge**. The Owner chooses a survivor and preferred Customer Details; Appointment links and observations move to it without snapshot changes; unique contact points and chronological Merchant Notes remain; consent evidence stays attached to its original destination, purpose, wording, source, and time; and the strictest active Customer Ban governs the survivor. The absorbed record becomes a non-searchable merge alias so old references resolve and the operation remains auditable. A mistaken merge is recovered through an explicit launch-time **Customer Record Split**, not by deleting the merge event: the Owner previews and assigns Appointment links, observations, contacts, and notes to a newly created record; consent follows its exact destination; snapshots remain unchanged; and any active ban initially governs both results until explicit review.

The **Customer Directory** searches preferred and previously observed names, normalized emails, and normalized phone numbers inside the current Merchant only. Ordinary results exclude archived records and absorbed aliases. Results show preferred details, possible-duplicate and ban state, last visit, next Appointment, and visit, No Show, and cancellation counts. Record detail presents chronological linked Appointments, Walk-in Queue and Waiting List activity, Merchant Notes, destination-specific consent evidence, merges, splits, and privacy actions. Appointment prices and External Collections remain truthful separate facts and are not presented as verified lifetime spend. Directory-wide free-text search excludes Merchant Notes.

The optional **Booking Note** is customer-supplied, Appointment-scoped, preserved in Appointment snapshots, and visible to the Owner and through that Appointment's Confirmation. It never becomes a general Customer Record note or structured intake response. A **Merchant Note** is private record-level text written by the Owner, never customer-visible, with authorship and created or edited times preserved in audit history. Neither kind of note may stand in for consent, a ban, or structured medical, payment, or intake data; the product warns against storing sensitive medical or payment information.

Communication state is destination-specific rather than one blanket contactable flag. Transactional email may use an address supplied for the booking without inferring Marketing Consent. Operational mobile messaging requires affirmative permission for the exact phone number. Consent Evidence records purpose, exact destination, wording or policy version, source, time, and withdrawal. A Customer Contact Preference may choose email, mobile messaging, both, or no optional reminders. Withdrawal immediately suppresses unsent messages to that destination, including reminders for existing Appointments. Merge never transfers permission to another destination, and retaining Marketing Consent evidence does not add out-of-scope launch marketing behavior.

A **Customer Ban** is Merchant-scoped and private, requires an auditable reason and actor, and is either indefinite or explicitly expires. A unique match blocks public booking, Waiting List entry, and Walk-in Queue entry; the public response is generic and neither reveals the ban nor confirms identity. Existing Appointments remain unchanged. The Owner may create an Appointment despite the ban only through an explicit override with a required reason. Lifting or expiry permits new activity but recreates nothing previously rejected or cancelled.

Archiving and erasure are separate. An **Archived Customer Record** is a reversible organization state hidden from ordinary search and reuse. It remains eligible for private exact-contact matching: a uniquely matched confirmed public booking restores it, while ambiguity creates a separate possible duplicate; Merchant creation requires explicit restore and selection. A ban always takes precedence. **Customer Data Erasure** irreversibly removes the record's preferred details, contact points, Merchant Notes, preferences, and private ban reason and anonymizes personal Customer Details in historical Appointments after applicable holds clear. Non-personal time, Service, price, lifecycle, collection, and audit facts remain. The erased record can never be restored or linked to later activity.

An erased customer may book again and create a genuinely new Customer Record; anonymous historical Appointments never relink. Old permission does not return. A retained withdrawal fingerprint suppresses the exact destination until fresh affirmative permission, and a surviving active-ban fingerprint continues to block until the ban ends. Minimal fingerprints cannot be used to contact or identify the person. Legal or dispute holds delay erasure transparently rather than recording a false completion.

Identifiable Customer Record and Appointment customer data expires thirty-six months after the latest customer interaction when there is no future Appointment, Waiting List or Walk-in Queue activity, active ban, or hold. Each new interaction restarts the clock, expiry automatically invokes Customer Data Erasure, and the Owner may request earlier erasure. Consent or withdrawal evidence remains for three years after permission ends or its last use, whichever is later, then only a necessary non-contactable suppression fingerprint may remain. An active ban fingerprint remains until the ban ends. Separate non-personal Appointment and finance retention still applies. The already-decided twelve-month Merchant-level deletion deadline after Restricted Access wins when shorter unless a valid hold applies; launch does not permit arbitrary retention extension.

Customer Directory Import accepts a versioned CSV with name, optional email, optional phone, and optional external reference. A dry run reports invalid rows, normalized values, exact matches, conflicts, and possible duplicates before commitment. An exact unique match may fill an empty preferred field but never overwrite one; ambiguous or conflicting rows remain possible duplicates; name-only rows are allowed but never matched automatically. File and row idempotency make retries safe. Import creates no Appointments, Booking Notes, Merchant Notes, bans, consent, or messaging permission, and the source file is retained only for processing and recovery.

The Owner may request a **Customer Directory Export** containing Customer Records, preferred and historical contacts, preferences, Merchant Notes, ban state, activity summaries, and stable Appointment references. BeeSolo generates an encrypted, short-lived download asynchronously, records an audit event, and never emails it automatically. A separate **Customer Data Package** serves a verified public Privacy Request for one record. It includes Customer Details observations, Booking Notes, Appointment and customer activity, preferences, and Consent Evidence; excludes Merchant Notes, private ban reasons, security signals, other customers' data, internal audit metadata, and secrets; represents merged records once with merge provenance; and can never resurrect erased values.

Access, correction, and erasure requests use BeeSolo's public **Privacy Request** surface without requiring a Customer Account or Merchant approval. The customer identifies the Merchant and proves control of an exact record email or phone through a one-time code. A Confirmation Access Token controls one Appointment only and cannot authorize a whole-record request. Shared destinations, ambiguous matches, and merge histories require System Operator review and proportionate additional verification. BeeSolo executes the request, audits each step, and gives the Merchant only the minimum necessary correction or erasure notice. The exact operator queue, permission, verification, hold, delivery, abuse-prevention, and recovery workflow belongs to [Define BeeSolo Privacy Request Operations](22-define-beesolo-privacy-request-operations.md).

A verified correction changes preferred details prospectively and marks an incorrect historical contact point disputed or superseded so it is excluded from matching, search results, and communication. It does not silently rewrite Appointment snapshots. When the personal value must be removed from historical snapshots, BeeSolo invokes the audited privacy-erasure mechanism while preserving non-personal facts. Existing Confirmation access and queued notifications follow the established Appointment destination-change security rules rather than changing implicitly across every linked Appointment.
