# Define Waiting List Operations

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 05, 09

## Question

What Merchant configuration, customer request, opening detection, baseline ordering, sequential offer, expiry, accept/decline/withdraw, conflict, Owner override, notification, and conversion-reporting rules turn the existing Waiting List capability into a complete BeeSolo operating loop without advanced ranking or optimization?

## Comments

### Resolution — 2026-07-31

BeeSolo has one Shop-level **Waiting List Admission** setting, an explicit subset of Active Services eligible for new applications, and one Shop-wide Active-application cap configurable from one through two hundred and defaulting to fifty. Admission and Service eligibility both default off. Effective new admission additionally requires a Published and Booking Ready Public Booking Page, subscription access that permits new bookings, capacity below the cap, and no currently bookable Time Slot matching the exact request. The admission command atomically rechecks those facts and returns current booking choices instead of creating an application when a matching slot exists.

Disabling admission, removing a Service from Waiting List eligibility, or lowering the cap affects only future admission; it never silently discards accepted demand. Making an underlying Service Inactive pauses offers for affected new-booking applications, while a replacement application follows its source Appointment snapshots and may retain an inactive Service under the time-only reschedule rule. Unpublishing, loss of Booking Readiness, Restricted Access, or another subscription gate pauses every new offer and supersedes Pending offers, but leaves applications inspectable and withdrawable. Restoring the relevant gate before application expiry resumes evaluation.

A new-booking **Waiting List Application** is one immutable request for one exact Primary Service and Additional Service set and one continuous Shop-local interval no longer than thirty-one calendar days and wholly inside the current Booking Horizon. The sole Owner-Provider is implicit and snapshotted; launch exposes no Provider preference. Admission snapshots the original Service names, durations, buffers, and prices for history but creates no quote. Later detection and checkout use current Active Service facts and current prices, and an invalid or Inactive combination pauses rather than silently substitutes Services. Changing Services, the requested interval, or application contact details requires withdrawal and a new application.

Admission requires a customer-facing name and valid email. Phone is optional Customer Directory data, not a Waiting List delivery channel at launch. In the admission transaction BeeSolo conservatively matches or creates and links a Merchant-scoped Customer Record under the existing exact-contact rules; a name alone never matches, ambiguity follows possible-duplicate behavior, and a uniquely matched active Customer Ban produces a generic rejection. One Active new-booking application may exist per uniquely matched Customer Record and Shop. Exact retry returns the existing protected application. A ban applied later supersedes any Pending offer and pauses further offers without exposing the reason; lifting it before expiry restores eligibility.

Public admission is immediate rather than email-verification-gated. Turnstile, IP, browser, and destination rate limits, generic rejection responses, atomic capacity allocation, and recipient-controlled withdrawal protect the surface. The required email confirmation carries **Waiting List Status Access**. That protected application view shows the exact request, lifecycle, deadline, current Pending offer, and Withdraw action; it shows no numeric position, wait estimate, other customer demand, private ban fact, subscription state, or detailed pause reason. Access remains valid while Active and for thirty days after terminal outcome. Rate-limited recovery sends a fresh secure link only to the exact application email, returns a generic public response, revokes prior status access, and cannot enumerate applications.

BeeSolo also retains the existing **Replacement Waiting List Application**. A customer starts it only through a Scheduled Appointment's protected Confirmation, selects one exact replacement interval, and keeps the original Appointment intact. At most one may be Active per source Appointment. It binds the source Appointment identity and revision and inherits its snapshotted Services, duration, buffers, and agreed price; it is a time-only replacement and cannot change Services. The source must remain Scheduled and customer-reschedulable at admission, offer, acceptance, and commit. An independent reschedule, Service edit, cancellation, completion, No Show, or passed customer-reschedule cutoff supersedes the application and its Pending offer. Acceptance opens a purpose-bound Reschedule Session, and only the atomic successful replacement fulfills the application; failure leaves the original Appointment unchanged.

The application lifecycle is **Active**, **Fulfilled**, **Withdrawn**, **Removed**, **Expired**, or **Superseded**. Only a confirmed new Appointment or atomically committed replacement makes it Fulfilled. Customer withdrawal is irreversible, supersedes a Pending offer, and requires a new application to rejoin. The authenticated Owner has a distinct **Remove Waiting List Application** command and cannot edit the customer-authored request. Removal reasons are Customer Requested Offline, Duplicate or Error, Abuse, Shop Unable to Serve, and Other; Other requires a private note. Removed is terminal and remains distinct from customer withdrawal. The requested interval's end expires the application, caps any Pending offer deadline, and requires a new application for later dates. Terminal applications never reopen.

Availability-changing commands enqueue Waiting List evaluation immediately with a target of issuing an eligible offer within one minute. A full recovery sweep runs every five minutes for time progression, expired Time Slot Holds, and missed wake-ups. Evaluation uses current authoritative Availability: Schedule Rules, working intervals, Date Overrides, Blocked Time, buffers, Booking Window, non-terminal Appointment conflicts, unexpired holds, publication and access gates, Customer Ban state, and purpose-specific Service facts. No-op scans are operational telemetry and create no Waiting List History.

New-booking and replacement applications share one deterministic FIFO ordered by application creation time and stable application ID. For each opening BeeSolo chooses the oldest eligible application; for each application it chooses the earliest eligible opening. Simultaneous allocation continues oldest-first over the earliest remaining openings. There is no score, priority class, fit optimization, customer-visible rank, or estimated wait. A **Waiting List Ordering Override** lets the Owner select a later eligible application for one specific opening only after supplying a private reason. It cannot change the request, bypass current eligibility or conflicts, extend deadlines, or create concurrent offers, and it preserves who was skipped and why in history.

One exact opening is offered sequentially to one application at a time. An application may have at most one Pending **Availability Offer**, and one exact Time Slot may have at most one Pending Waiting List offer. A Pending offer prevents another Waiting List offer for that slot but creates neither an Appointment nor a Time Slot Hold and never outranks ordinary public booking or an Owner Appointment command. Its response window is a fixed fifteen minutes from server issuance, capped by application expiry. Decline or expiry advances a still-available slot immediately to the next eligible application. Accepting and then abandoning conversion advances it after the ten-minute hold releases. Every attempted application-and-slot pair is final: that exact slot is never offered to that application again, while the application keeps its original FIFO age for different openings.

Accepting an offer atomically revalidates the application, offer, Customer Ban, publication and subscription gates, purpose-specific Service facts, current Availability, and conflict set. For a new booking it creates a constrained Booking Session with the exact offered time, sole Owner-Provider, and Service set locked, a ten-minute Time Slot Hold, current pricing, prefilled but correctable Customer Details, the optional Booking Note, and the ordinary Pay In Person confirmation flow. A replacement acceptance creates the equivalent purpose-bound Reschedule Session using the source Appointment snapshots and agreed price. An accepted application receives no further offers while conversion remains live, but it is not Fulfilled until commit. Explicit abandonment, hold or session expiry, slot loss, or failed confirmation returns it to Active unless withdrawal, removal, expiry, supersession, or fulfillment has already won.

Declining rejects only that offer and leaves the application Active without penalty. If public booking, an Owner command, a Schedule Change, a hold race, or another current fact takes the slot before acceptance, a Pending offer becomes Superseded. If the customer already accepted, conversion returns `slot_lost`. In either case the application returns to Active and the customer sees the opening is unavailable without losing FIFO age. No path creates overlapping non-terminal Appointments, and no stale offer or session may bypass current policy.

Every customer, Owner, and Worker mutation carries an idempotency key and expected application or offer revision as applicable. Admission uniqueness and capacity, one Pending offer per application, one Pending Waiting List offer per exact slot, offer response, conversion pause, and terminal transitions are enforced atomically. Accept, decline, withdraw, remove, expiry, supersession, opening allocation, public booking, and Owner scheduling races use first-commit-wins. A stale loser receives current authorized state and changes nothing. Retry cannot duplicate Customer links, history, offers, Notification Intents, Booking Sessions, Time Slot Holds, Appointments, or reschedules.

**Waiting List History** records every successful admission, offer issuance and outcome, response, ordering override, withdrawal, removal, conversion, expiry, and supersession. Each entry preserves actor or Background Worker provenance, any impersonating System Operator, server time, prior and resulting revision, original and changed facts, required reason, and linked Notification Intent, Booking Session, hold, source Appointment, and resulting Appointment identities. Derived evaluation and no-op scans do not pollute this business history.

Only the authenticated Owner may configure admission, inspect all applications and exact pause reasons, remove an application, or perform an ordering override. An Impersonated Merchant Session receives that effective Owner authority while preserving full System Operator provenance; a Merchant Access Hold, revoked authentication, or invalid impersonation still blocks it. Customers receive only application-scoped inspection and withdrawal plus offer-scoped acceptance or decline. The Background Worker receives only evaluation, offer issuance, offer and application expiry, gate or conflict supersession, conversion recovery, and replacement-source invalidation authority.

Waiting List delivery is email-only at BeeSolo launch because the authoritative Operational Messaging Router explicitly excludes Availability Offer and Waiting List mobile events from its launch event set. Admission creates one immediate confirmation email intent with status access. Offer issuance atomically creates one urgent email intent and starts the fifteen-minute deadline; provider delay, retry, or failure neither rolls back nor extends the offer, and retries stop when the offer is no longer current. Application expiry sends one closure email stating that no Appointment was created. Decline and withdrawal send none because the customer just performed the action. Offer supersession and `slot_lost` use the protected or live conversion view without a separate email. Removal sends email only for Shop Unable to Serve or Other, with optional customer-safe wording. Fulfillment relies only on the ordinary Appointment confirmation or reschedule notification and never sends a duplicate Waiting List success message. Notification delivery failure never rolls back a Waiting List or Appointment command.

The Owner receives no outbound email for each application or offer. The Merchant App shows Active demand, derived pause reasons, capacity, offer progress, delivery outcomes, removals, overrides, and required actions; repeated Worker failure or capacity pressure belongs in in-app operational state and platform monitoring. Exact layout and responsive interaction remain with [Prototype the BeeSolo Merchant Control Plane](15-prototype-merchant-control-plane.md), while exact templates, email retries, provider outcomes, and notification recovery remain with [Define Transactional Notification Workflows](11-define-transactional-notification-workflows.md).

Waiting List reporting groups immutable historical facts by Shop-local period and selected Service. It includes applications admitted and terminal outcomes by Fulfilled, Withdrawn, Removed reason, Expired, and Superseded; offers issued and outcomes by Accepted, Declined, Expired, and Superseded; application-to-confirmed-Appointment conversion; offer acceptance and accepted-offer-to-confirmation conversion; median and p90 application-to-first-offer, offer-to-accept, and accept-to-confirm durations; ordering-override counts; removal reasons; and email delivery outcomes kept separate from domain outcomes. Application conversion uses admission-date cohorts, shows still-Active demand separately, and becomes final only for matured cohorts whose applications are terminal; offer metrics use offer-issuance periods. Original Customer and Service snapshots prevent later edits from rewriting historical periods. Appointment Price Snapshots and External Collections remain separate operational facts and are never labelled Waiting List revenue.

The existing Customer Directory privacy, retention, erasure, legal-hold, and Merchant-deletion rules govern identifiable Waiting List data. Active Waiting List activity delays ordinary customer inactivity erasure as already specified; erasure removes or anonymizes personal values without deleting non-personal lifecycle, ordering, latency, and aggregate reporting facts. This resolution adds no ranking, optimization, recurring preference, Team Provider choice, mobile Waiting List messaging, or new implementation ticket. Exact schemas, Effect contracts, migrations, worker slices, compatibility handling, and rollout sequencing remain with the existing notification, control-plane, reporting, release-gate, and implementation-program tickets.
