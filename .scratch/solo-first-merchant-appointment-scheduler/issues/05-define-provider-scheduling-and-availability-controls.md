# Define Provider Scheduling and Availability Controls

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

What Owner-controlled Schedule Rules, hours, date exceptions, block time, buffers, minimum notice, booking horizon, timezone behavior, and Service eligibility must produce Availability for BeeSolo's sole Owner-Provider while preserving conflict-safe holds, appointment commitments, DST correctness, and historical Provider facts?

## Comments

### Resolution — 2026-07-30

BeeSolo exposes one Schedule for its one Shop and sole Owner-Provider. The Owner-Provider remains Active and is automatically eligible for every Active Service; launch exposes no Provider selector, Provider administration, or editable Service-eligibility matrix. Inactive Services cannot enter a new Booking Session, while existing Booking Quotes, Appointments, Confirmations, and historical Provider references remain valid through their immutable snapshots.

Schedule Rules have four distinct inputs. **Weekly Working Hours** are recurring Shop-local intervals grouped by weekday. A weekday may have multiple non-overlapping intervals, saved explicitly in five-minute increments, and no interval may cross midnight. **Date Overrides** replace rather than merge with Weekly Working Hours for one Shop-local date; an override either closes the date or provides one or more non-overlapping intervals. **Blocked Time** is a one-off exact instant interval with five-minute input increments, may cross midnight or span dates, and may carry an optional Merchant-private reason. Launch has no recurring Blocked Time: recurring breaks use split Weekly Working Hours and date-specific closures use Date Overrides. The Shop-level **Start-Time Interval** is 5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes, defaults to 15, and controls the candidate-start grid independently of Service duration.

Schedule configuration never silently creates default hours. Merchant Activation requires at least one explicit Weekly Working Hours interval and explicit review of Date Overrides across the configured Booking Horizon. A Date Override takes precedence over Weekly Working Hours for its date. Availability then considers starts on the Start-Time Interval grid and keeps only candidates whose entire occupied interval fits inside one effective working interval. It subtracts Blocked Time, Scheduled Appointment occupied intervals, and unexpired competing Time Slot Holds; terminal Appointments do not block. It also requires the Public Booking Page, subscription access, active Service facts, and the sole Owner-Provider's automatic eligibility to be currently bookable. Generated Availability and Time Slots are never persisted.

Each Service has independently configurable before and after **Service Buffers** from 0 through 120 minutes in five-minute increments, both defaulting to zero. Buffers reserve Provider setup or cleanup time without increasing the customer-facing Service duration. For a Primary Service with Additional Services, customer-facing Service durations remain contiguous; the Appointment occupied interval uses the largest selected before buffer and largest selected after buffer rather than summing buffers or inserting hidden gaps. Availability conflicts use that occupied interval. Time Slot Holds, Booking Quotes, and Appointments snapshot the resolved buffer and occupied-interval facts so later catalog changes cannot rewrite history.

The **Booking Window** combines Minimum Notice and Booking Horizon. Minimum Notice is an exact elapsed duration from the current instant, including across daylight-saving transitions, configurable from 0 minutes through 30 days in fifteen-minute increments. Booking Horizon is calendar-based in the Shop timezone, configurable from 1 through 365 whole days; a value of N includes every otherwise valid start on the local date N days after today. Minimum Notice must be shorter than the nominal Booking Horizon. The activation preset remains two hours of Minimum Notice and a 60-day Booking Horizon. Availability display and Time Slot Hold acquisition both enforce the current Booking Window, and hold acquisition atomically revalidates every current scheduling and conflict fact.

Weekly Working Hours, Date Overrides, and the Start-Time Interval are civil-time rules anchored to the Shop's IANA timezone. On daylight-saving changes, nonexistent local starts are omitted and repeated local times may yield two distinct Time Slots; Booking and Merchant surfaces distinguish repeated times with their UTC offset or timezone abbreviation. Appointments, Blocked Time, and Time Slot Holds are exact instant intervals and never shift because of DST. The Owner may change the Shop timezone after an impact preview and explicit confirmation, but only when no Time Slot Hold is active. The change preserves Weekly Working Hours and Date Overrides as the same local labels under the new timezone, preserves Appointment and Blocked Time instants, re-derives future Availability, and never rewrites historical facts.

A Time Slot Hold is provisional rather than an Appointment commitment. Except for the guarded timezone change, an Owner mutation to Schedule Rules, Booking Window controls, Service duration, or Service Buffers takes effect immediately and atomically invalidates only the holds it makes unavailable. An affected customer receives `slot_lost` on the next action, keeps the rest of the Booking Party selections, and returns to refreshed Availability; no separate notification is sent because no Appointment exists. Unaffected holds and their immutable quotes remain valid. This deliberately supersedes the parent effort's earlier rule that later scheduling changes could never affect a valid hold, as recorded in [Hold a Time Slot and freeze the Booking Quote](../../booking-product-recreation/issues/18-hold-time-slot-and-freeze-booking-quote.md).

An Owner may save Weekly Working Hours, Date Override, Blocked Time, Booking Window, Service-duration, or buffer changes even when they make an existing future Appointment fall outside current Availability. The save requires an impact preview and explicit confirmation, takes effect for new bookings immediately, and never moves or cancels the Appointment. Each affected non-terminal Appointment exposes a derived **Schedule Conflict** until it is rescheduled, cancelled, or otherwise reaches a terminal state. This includes Blocked Time intentionally placed over an Appointment during an emergency.

Only current scheduling configuration is authoritative for new Availability. Every Schedule Change records immutable actor, timestamp, optional reason, and before-and-after audit facts, while accepted Appointment and unaffected hold snapshots preserve booking history. BeeSolo does not retain queryable Schedule Rule versions, persist generated Availability, or promise to reconstruct every Time Slot previously shown.
