# Define Transactional Notification Workflows

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 06, 09, 12, 13, 21

## Question

Which customer and Merchant Owner events produce email, SMS, or both; how do Merchant templates, timing, timezone, channel preference, consent and opt-out, localization, version-bound reminder invalidation, retries, dead letters, replay, deduplication, delivery history, and disabled-provider states work across appointment, Walk-in Queue, and Waiting List lifecycles?

## Comments

### Resolution

Transactional Email is required BeeSolo infrastructure rather than a Merchant-selectable event matrix. Appointment confirmation, reschedule, cancellation, and the required Reminder create customer email work; Appointment Series creation and Cancel Remaining Series consolidate once per unique destination, while later changes and reminders remain Appointment-specific. A destination correction mandates a current-revision confirmation and rebuilds any future Reminder. Completion, No Show, outcome correction, External Collection, and other internal status changes create none. Merchant creation, reschedule, cancellation, series creation, and remaining-series cancellation retain the existing explicit **Don't Notify** exception with a private reason when the customer already knows; it suppresses the system consequence and is not a manual messaging facility.

Waiting List messaging is email-only. Admission sends status access, offer issuance sends the urgent offer without extending its fifteen-minute deadline, application expiry sends closure, and Owner removal sends only for Shop Unable to Serve or Other. Decline, withdrawal, offer supersession, `slot_lost`, and fulfillment create no separate Waiting List message; fulfillment relies on the ordinary Appointment notification. The existing generic, rate-limited exact-email status-access recovery is a deterministic system recovery transition, not an ad hoc resend.

Walk-in messaging is email-only when an eligible email exists. Enrollment sends status access, Call sends the urgent spot assignment, Return to Waiting after Call sends a correction, and Owner removal sends only for Shop Unable to Serve or Other. Position or estimate changes, Customer Left, No Response, Duplicate or Error, expiry, Serving, Served, and other queue activity create none. Owner-assisted status-access recovery remains an in-person capability reissue rather than a transactional message.

Mobile Operational Messaging remains the authoritative router's Appointment-only launch set: confirmation, reschedule, cancellation, and Reminder. It is additive to essential email, routes WhatsApp first with SMS fallback, and is never exposed as a customer channel chooser. Waiting List and Walk-in events do not use mobile at launch. The sole mobile confirmation may carry Confirmation access; other mobile purposes carry no generic resource links. Messaging Balance and charge evidence remain independent of email.

Owner outbound messaging is email-only at launch and limited to a customer-confirmed Appointment, customer reschedule, customer cancellation, and Low Messaging Balance Notice. Owner commands, reminders, individual delivery failures, Waiting List activity, and Walk-in activity create no Owner email; no Owner mobile channel or global Merchant message center is introduced.

Only BeeSolo's deterministic notification engine creates and submits transactional messages in response to qualifying domain commits, scheduled due times, or declared recovery transitions. Merchant Owners, customers, and System Operators cannot compose, resend, replay, or manually create replacement messages. Operations may reconcile an ambiguous provider submission or recover a provably lost pre-submission wake-up of the same live intent, but terminal work remains terminal. A legitimate source mutation—such as correcting a destination or rescheduling—creates a new source revision from which the system alone decides the required new consequence.

Supplying an email for a workflow authorizes its essential transactional email without a separate checkbox or marketing-style unsubscribe; Marketing Consent remains unrelated. A public future Appointment requires valid email. An Owner-created Appointment may omit email only through an explicit recorded No-Contact Exception; its Reminder then uses eligible mobile or becomes **Not Sent — no eligible destination**. Walk-ins that begin immediately and retrospective completed visits have no Reminder. Essential reminders cannot be disabled or individually opted out.

Mobile requires affirmative permission for the exact phone number and request. Owner-created Appointments default to no mobile and may use immutable evidence of the Owner recording the customer's verbal or in-person agreement to the standardized disclosure, including wording, source, actor, and time. Selecting a Customer Record never implies permission. **Stop text updates** withdraws WhatsApp and SMS permission for that exact phone across the Shop, stops unsent and future mobile work, does not affect email or other Shops, cannot recall provider-accepted work, and requires fresh affirmative permission before future mobile use. The Owner cannot silently clear the withdrawal.

BeeSolo owns versioned Romanian and English templates. Merchants cannot author subjects, markup, links, or arbitrary bodies; rendering accepts authoritative workflow facts and explicitly bounded customer-safe fields only. Spanish and French are hidden at launch rather than silently falling back for notifications. Customer-started work snapshots the selected Romanian or English interface language. Owner-created or assisted work requires an explicit customer language prefilled from the Shop default and never inferred from name, phone country, browser locale, or Customer Record history.

Each Scheduled Appointment has exactly one required Reminder. The Owner chooses 2, 24, or 48 hours before start, defaulting to 24 hours, with no Off setting; email and eligible mobile share the same target. Reminder delivery is restricted to 08:00–20:00 in the source revision's snapshotted Shop IANA timezone. A target before 08:00 shifts earlier to 20:00 on the previous day; a target after 20:00 shifts earlier to 20:00 that day. A target exactly at a boundary is valid. Thus an 08:00 Appointment with a 24-hour policy sends at 08:00 the prior day, while its 2-hour target of 06:00 shifts to 20:00 on the preceding evening. Reminder work never catches up after Appointment start. Reschedule supersedes the prior reminder and creates a replacement only while its new target is still useful; cancellation terminates reminder work. Every non-reminder notification becomes due at domain commit regardless of this window, and provider delay never extends a workflow deadline.

Composite Booking Party confirmation and atomic whole-party cancellation consolidate once per unique email destination and disclose only that destination's affected Appointments; the coordinating Customer receives the protected whole-party Confirmation. Different guest destinations receive only their own Appointment. Mobile permission remains request-specific. Later changes and reminders remain Appointment-specific.

Changing an Appointment email revokes old Confirmation access, supersedes unsent old-destination work, mandates a system-issued current-revision confirmation to the new email, and rebuilds any future Reminder. It cannot use **Don't Notify**. Removing email requires the No-Contact Exception. Mobile permission never transfers to another phone number. Appointment emails carry current Confirmation access; Waiting List email carries only its status or offer access; Walk-in email carries only its status access. URLs contain no raw resource identifiers or customer data.

The Merchant Public Booking Page exposes **Browser Active Bookings** from opaque Appointment access capabilities retained on that device and browser. Successful booking records the capabilities, and opening a valid Confirmation link records that Appointment in another browser. Only active Appointments appear. Clearing browser data or changing device loses that list; BeeSolo provides no public Appointment search and sends no Appointment recovery notification. This browser continuity does not change the independent lifetime of Confirmation access.

One semantic Notification Operation is committed atomically with the source transition and groups independent email and mobile Notification Intents. Its automatic deduplication key is Shop, source type, source identity and revision, purpose, recipient, and delivery family; consolidated series or party email resolves once per destination. Provider retries add attempts to the same intent. Source changes atomically mark unsent stale work **Not Sent — superseded** and create only required current work. Provider-accepted or Submission Unknown work becomes superseded-after-submission, stops further retry or mobile fallback, and continues receiving evidence without pretending recall.

Email uses a write-ahead Submission Attempt and stable provider idempotency key. Explicitly safe transient failure may retry; a timeout or lost response becomes **Submission Unknown**, permits reconciliation only, and never blind resend. Urgent Waiting List offer and Walk-in spot email tries when due and at +30 seconds, +2 minutes, +5 minutes, and +10 minutes, stopping when its state or deadline is stale. Other lifecycle email tries when due and at +1 minute, +5 minutes, +15 minutes, +1 hour, +4 hours, and +12 hours; Reminder retries additionally stop at Appointment start. Every attempt revalidates source revision, destination, suppression, access, template availability, and purpose validity. Permanent rejection never retries, and `Retry-After` is honored only inside the remaining useful lifetime.

Provider API success means **Provider Accepted**, not Delivered. Only trustworthy provider evidence establishes **Delivered**; later bounce or complaint evidence refines the visible result without rewriting attempt history. Hard bounce suppresses the exact email. Complaint suppresses it and enters Operations review. Affected active Appointments show **Customer email needs attention**; due work without a valid destination becomes Not Sent. Correcting the Appointment email is the domain transition that lets the system issue the mandatory current confirmation and rebuild a future Reminder.

Permanent rejection, exhausted retry, or unresolved submission ambiguity creates terminal **Delivery Failed** plus a redacted dead letter. Neither the dead letter nor terminal intent is replayed or reopened. Merchant delivery history is a source-local grouped timeline showing purpose, time, locale, masked destination, independent email and mobile results, safe attempt times, and safe failure reasons. It is observational and has no resend action. Provider payloads and raw identifiers remain Operations-only evidence.

Notification Readiness requires a test Appointment email to the Owner's verified account to be provider-accepted. Known **Needs Configuration** or **Disabled** email state makes live readiness false and pauses new public demand while preserving the Merchant's Published intent; existing commitments and Owner commands remain operable. Due work in those states becomes terminal Not Sent with the exact reason and is not replayed after restoration. A configured transient outage uses ordinary retries. Mobile never substitutes for email readiness. The Merchant App shows persistent degraded configuration state rather than per-message Owner alerts.

Each intent binds immutable template version, locale, source facts, access-link identity, and safe fingerprints without retaining a long-lived rendered body. Ordinary template retirement affects only new work. A safety, legal, provider, or correctness disablement terminates bound pending work as **Not Sent — template unavailable**; only a later qualifying system transition can bind a current template.

Encrypted destinations remain while actionable plus thirty days after terminal, capped at ninety days after submission without a narrow hold. Provider attempts, normalized evidence, and controlled rendering facts remain 180 days; redacted ordinary logs remain thirty days. Masked timeline summary follows the source record and Customer Data Erasure. Consent Evidence retains its separate three-year rule, mobile financial evidence follows statutory retention, and rendered message bodies are never retained long-term.

These decisions supersede the earlier broad map statement that SMS covers queue lifecycles, the optional fresh-link behavior for Appointment destination edits in ticket 06, and any implied customer Reminder opt-out or manual **Fresh Notification Send**. Exact schemas, Effect contracts, migrations, worker slices, UI components, compatibility handling, rollout sequencing, and release evidence remain with the downstream reporting, release-gate, and implementation-program tickets. Ticket 16 is now unblocked; no additional investigation ticket is required.
