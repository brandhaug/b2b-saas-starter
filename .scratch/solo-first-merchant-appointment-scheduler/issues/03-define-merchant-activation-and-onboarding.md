# Define Merchant Activation and Onboarding

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 02

## Question

What is the shortest complete Merchant Onboarding journey from account creation and Merchant Subscription selection to a tested, Published Public Booking Page, including Solo Owner-as-Provider setup, Shop details, services, working hours and exceptions, booking policies, notification readiness, preview, recovery, progress persistence, and the distinct Team branch?

## Comments

### Resolution — 2026-07-29

Merchant Onboarding begins with account creation and email verification. A verified person with no Merchant selects Solo or Team and a monthly or annual billing interval before the creation boundary. One atomic command then creates the Merchant, Merchant Owner membership, the Owner's Active Provider, a hidden default Brand, exactly one Shop and Shop Address, an Unpublished Public Booking Page, and the Merchant Subscription in its single 14-day Trialing period. No card is required. Abandoning before that command creates no Merchant and starts no trial; retrying the command is idempotent and can never create a second Merchant or trial.

After creation, onboarding is a resumable, dependency-aware checklist rather than a modal wizard. Its canonical order is Business Details and Owner-Provider confirmation, Team Setup when applicable, Service setup, working hours and exception review, Booking Policies, Notification Readiness, Preview Mode and Launch Test, then publication. The Owner may leave onboarding and use authenticated Trialing features whose own prerequisites are satisfied. Required progress is derived from authoritative domain configuration rather than manually stored completion flags. Valid logical sections persist through explicit Save or Save and continue actions with optimistic concurrency, idempotency, and visible saving, saved, and failed states; failed saves retain current-browser input for retry, while the last successful server state remains authoritative across devices. Completed steps remain editable and returning Owners resume at the first incomplete required step.

Business Details is the UI grouping for the existing single-Shop topology, not a second onboarding aggregate. The UI hides Brand and multi-Shop concepts while editing the Merchant identity, one persisted Shop and Shop Address, and Public Booking Page presentation. Launch supports one fixed physical premises only. Activation requires country, street address, city or locality, postal code where applicable, IANA timezone, and a public phone number. Website or social link and arrival directions are optional. Mobile services, customer-address visits, virtual appointments, multiple service locations, and Brand management are outside this destination.

For Solo, the automatically created Owner-Provider remains Active, takes its initial customer-facing name from the verified account, and requires only confirmation or editing of that name. Photo, bio, pronouns, and specialties are optional. The Owner-Provider becomes eligible automatically for every new Service. Merchant Activation requires at least one Active Service with customer-facing name, a selected or inline-created category, a duration in five-minute increments from five minutes through eight hours, and a positive price in the Shop currency; description and image are optional, and the Owner may add more Services before continuing.

Schedule setup starts empty and never silently assumes business hours. Activation requires at least one explicit weekly working interval for an eligible Provider in the Shop's IANA timezone. A day may have multiple non-overlapping intervals, one day may be copied to others, and overnight intervals are excluded. Date-specific exceptions may close a day or replace its normal intervals. Creating an exception is optional, but the Owner must explicitly confirm that upcoming closures have been reviewed.

Booking Policies begin with a prefilled launch preset that the Owner must confirm or edit: customers may book from two hours through sixty days ahead; customers may cancel or reschedule until twenty-four hours before the Appointment; after the cutoff they contact the business through its required public phone; successful bookings confirm automatically. Pay In Person implies no cancellation fee, no no-show charge, no stored payment credential, and no copy suggesting otherwise. These choices become versioned policy inputs and customer-facing disclosures.

Transactional email is mandatory for Merchant Activation. Notification Readiness requires a test appointment email sent to the Owner's verified account email and accepted by the configured provider. Customer messages use the platform's verified no-reply sender and require no Merchant reply-to address. A development adapter may satisfy the check outside production, while production requires the configured delivery provider. SMS is optional, independently funded, explicitly enabled by the Owner, and never blocks activation.

Preview Mode rehearses the real customer path against current saved configuration and production booking and Availability rules: Service, Provider preference, an actual available Time Slot, customer details, review, and simulated confirmation. It creates no operational Appointment or Customer Directory entry, consumes no Time Slot, and sends no customer notification. A successful Launch Test is recorded against the activation-relevant configuration and becomes stale if that configuration changes before first publication. Retesting is not required after first publication.

Booking Readiness remains the narrow derived publication condition already established: public name and slug, an Active Service, an eligible Active Provider, and Schedule Rules. Merchant Activation is the separate one-time first-publication condition that composes Booking Readiness with completed Business Details, confirmed Booking Policies, Notification Readiness, and a current Launch Test. The final Publish command atomically and idempotently re-evaluates Merchant Activation and Subscription Access. Failure leaves the page Unpublished, preserves all configuration, identifies every failed check, links to its step, and permits retry. Success makes the page Published, permanently records first activation, closes onboarding, and takes the Owner to the Merchant App dashboard with the public URL. Later edits do not reopen onboarding; ongoing customer availability depends on current Booking Readiness, Public Page Status, and Subscription Access.

Team follows the shared path through Business Details, then inserts Team Setup before Services. At least one invitation must be accepted so the Merchant has Team's minimum two Active Merchant Members; pending invitations may be resent or revoked but do not satisfy activation. Each accepted member receives a fixed role and an explicit, separate Provider-linkage decision. The Owner-Provider begins Active; newly accepted members begin without Provider linkage. The Owner may become non-bookable only after another Active Provider satisfies Service eligibility and schedule requirements. Shared setup then continues with Team-aware eligibility and schedules. Detailed invitation, role, recovery, revocation, and Provider-linkage behavior remains owned by Define Team Membership, Roles, and Provider Linkage.

During Trialing, Solo-to-Team takes effect immediately and inserts Team Setup without resetting shared progress or the trial clock. Team-to-Solo takes effect immediately only after pending invitations are revoked, additional memberships are removed or inactive, and the Owner is again the sole Active Provider; paid plan changes follow the already settled subscription lifecycle. If trial or paid access becomes Restricted before activation, all saved configuration and checklist results remain available read-only, setup mutations and publication stop, and billing recovery becomes the primary action. Successful payment restores the same Merchant automatically and resumes at the first incomplete step. A prior Launch Test remains valid unless activation-relevant configuration changed.

### Scope amendment — 2026-07-29

BeeSolo onboarding is Solo-only. It offers monthly or annual Solo billing and follows Business Details and Owner-Provider confirmation, Services, working hours and exception review, Booking Policies, Notification Readiness, Launch Test, and publication. Team selection, Team Setup, invitations, additional Providers, and plan-switch transitions are deferred and must not appear in the launch flow.
