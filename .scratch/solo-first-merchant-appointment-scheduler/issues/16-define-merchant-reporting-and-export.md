# Define Merchant Reporting and Export

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 06, 09, 11, 12, 13

## Question

What definitions, timezones, filters, Owner permissions, drill-downs, CSV schemas, retention rules, and empty/error states should govern BeeSolo launch reporting for appointment volume and status, cancellations, No Shows, rebooking, Owner-Provider utilization, Walk-in wait time, Waiting List conversion, notification failures, and manually recorded External Collections without presenting unverified collection records as platform revenue?

## Comments

### Resolution — 2026-08-01

BeeSolo reporting uses the Shop's configured IANA **Shop Reporting Timezone** for every period, civil-time bucket, lead-time calculation, report view, and export. Browser or device timezone never changes membership. Every report and export identifies the timezone and handles daylight-saving transitions as local civil time. Reports default to the last thirty Shop-local calendar days including today and offer Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This month, Last month, Year to date, and Custom; Custom may cover any retained period.

The reporting contract has two date attributions. The primary service-period view groups each distinct Appointment by its scheduled start in the Shop timezone and counts it once using its latest status. The activity-period view groups cancellations, No Shows, completions, and outcome corrections by the timestamp at which that activity occurred. Thus an Appointment scheduled for August 2 and cancelled on August 1 appears in the August 2 outcome report and the August 1 activity report.

The common filter set is the required date range plus optional Service selection. Appointment reports additionally filter by current status and Appointment origin: Public Booking, Merchant-created, Walk-in conversion, or Waiting List conversion. There are no Shop or Provider filters at Solo launch. Collection method is a detail and export field, not a global filter.

Appointment volume counts each distinct Appointment whose scheduled start falls in the selected period exactly once. Series members count individually. Booking Sessions, Time Slot Holds, Waiting List Applications, Walk-in Entries, and Notification Intents never count as Appointments. Status counts are mutually exclusive: Scheduled, Completed, Cancelled, and No Show. For service-period cohorts with terminal outcomes, Cancellation Rate is `Cancelled / (Cancelled + Completed + No Show)` and No Show Rate is `No Show / (Completed + No Show)`; still-Scheduled Appointments are shown separately and excluded from both denominators. A Rebooking is a new, non-Cancelled Appointment for the same Customer Record, starting after a Completed Appointment and within ninety calendar days. Rebooking Rate is calculated only after that ninety-day follow-up window has matured and excludes same-Booking-Party and same-Series members.

Owner-Provider utilization reports both Capacity Utilization and Service Utilization against bookable minutes from Schedule Rules after Date Overrides and Blocked Time. Capacity Utilization uses occupied Appointment intervals including resolved Service Buffers for Scheduled, Completed, and No Show Appointments. Service Utilization uses actual or planned service minutes for Completed Appointments. Cancellations do not consume utilization; No Shows consume capacity and remain separately visible. Outside-hours or explicitly overridden Appointments count in the numerator and may produce utilization above 100% rather than being hidden.

Walk-in wait is measured from Walk-in Enrollment to actual Start Walk-in Service. The report includes median, p90, and maximum wait for entries that reach service; Removed and Expired entries are excluded from wait-time calculations but counted as terminal outcomes. Estimate error against actual start, Start-to-Finish service duration, queue length, and terminal outcomes remain separate measures.

Waiting List reporting is an admission-date cohort funnel: applications admitted, offers issued, offers accepted, confirmed Appointments, and terminal outcomes by reason. For matured cohorts, Application Conversion is confirmed Appointments divided by terminal applications. Still-Active applications are visible but excluded from the denominator. New-booking and Replacement Waiting List Applications are separate, with offer-acceptance and accept-to-confirmation durations alongside conversion.

Notification delivery is a separate operational report grouped by purpose, channel, delivery outcome, and delivery or attempt period. It distinguishes Delivered, Provider Accepted, Delivery Failed, Not Sent, Submission Unknown, and suppression or invalid-destination outcomes. Notification outcomes never change Appointment, Waiting List, or Walk-in conversion metrics. The Owner sees aggregate counts and source-level actionable attention flags such as **Customer contact needs attention**, with masked destinations and safe reasons; provider payloads, raw identifiers, and manual resend remain Operations-only.

Financial reporting never presents platform revenue or Payment totals for Pay In Person. It keeps Appointment Price Snapshot, net External Collections, and Returned External Collections separate. External Collections are Owner-recorded off-platform amounts and are explicitly unverified; an indicated difference from the Appointment Price Snapshot is not verified debt or revenue. Amounts use the Shop's single EUR currency and are labelled as agreed value or net External Collection rather than revenue or Payment.

Only the authenticated Merchant Owner may view reports and exports. An Impersonated Merchant Session may exercise the same effective authority with System Operator provenance. Customers, public sessions, deferred Team roles, and Platform API credentials receive no reporting access. Restricted Access preserves read and export; a Merchant Access Hold blocks access.

Every aggregate drills down only to underlying Merchant-scoped records within the selected filters. Appointment detail includes local times, status or outcome, Service snapshot, origin, Customer Details, and External Collection entries. Walk-in and Waiting List detail includes lifecycle facts and linked Appointments. Notification detail includes purpose, masked destination, outcome, safe failure reason, and attempt times. Other customers' identities or queue positions, raw provider payloads, credentials, and cross-Merchant data are never exposed.

Reporting Export mirrors the selected report rather than producing a duplicated mega-file: one CSV per primary fact—Appointments, External Collection entries, Walk-in Entries, Waiting List Applications or Offers, and Notification Intents or delivery families. Every file includes stable opaque identifiers for its row and permitted related records, including Merchant-scoped Customer Record IDs, but no customer name, email, phone, Merchant Note, ban reason, consent evidence, confirmation token, or raw notification destination. Customer contact data remains in Customer Directory Export. Export metadata states Shop, period, Shop Reporting Timezone, currency, filters, and generation time. Exact instants include both ISO-8601 UTC and equivalent Shop-local timestamps; money uses two-decimal major units plus ISO currency. Reports and exports use one server-side consistent read and generated-at instant, so later commits appear only after refresh or a new export.

Report views derive from authoritative source facts on demand and do not create a permanent aggregate copy. Encrypted generated exports remain available for twenty-four hours and are then deleted; export audit metadata follows ordinary governance retention, while source records retain their own domain retention and privacy-erasure behavior. A valid query with no matches is an explicit zero or empty state and can produce a header-only CSV. Invalid dates or filters are validation errors. Source or system failure is an unavailable report with retry, never fabricated zero or silently partial data. Pending Notification Evidence remains pending rather than failed, and a failed export creates no downloadable artifact.
