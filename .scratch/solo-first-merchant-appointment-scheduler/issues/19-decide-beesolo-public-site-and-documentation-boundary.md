# Decide the beesolo Public Site and Documentation Boundary

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

Which Public Site pages and checked-in MDX documents should beesolo publish at launch; which residual B2B SaaS Starter, Showcase Site, Reference Application, Workspace, Starter Module, and Adoption Readiness content must be rewritten, redirected, hidden, archived, or removed; and what naming, Solo-only product claims, legal copy, navigation, search, metadata, and regression checks ensure no public surface advertises deferred Team behavior or the retired repository-showcase product?

## Comments

### Resolution — 2026-08-01

The customer-facing product name is always **beesolo** in lowercase, including headings, sentence starts, navigation, metadata, manifests, structured data, social graphics, internal package names, imports, and deployable resource identity. Active `b2b-saas-starter` and `@b2b-saas-starter/*` identifiers migrate to `beesolo` and `@beesolo/*`; persisted or deployed resource identifiers require an explicit compatibility and cutover plan rather than destructive recreation. Until a separate brand asset is approved, the Public Site uses a lowercase text-only `beesolo` wordmark and removes the starter Boxes icon, favicon, manifest identity, and social graphics without inventing a replacement logo.

The Public Site serves prospective solopreneur barbers and salon professionals and their customers. It does not present a repository showcase, starter template, reference application, architecture guide, module catalog, adoption-readiness system, or external-developer portal. Active runtime code, public content, assets, fixtures, README/setup guidance, and current architecture documentation must lose the retired starter identity. Historically useful ADRs and research may retain old terms only when clearly marked superseded and excluded from public builds and search; architectural history is not deleted wholesale.

The complete launch route set is the localized Home, Pricing, Blog index and article, Help index and article, FAQ, Privacy, Terms, and the privacy-request intake reserved by **Define beesolo Privacy Request Operations**, plus effectively available Merchant Public Booking Pages and their Booking App journeys. Merchant sign-in and trial creation are cross-surface calls to action. There is no standalone Features page: Home owns the product overview. `/docs/**`, `/changelog`, and all legacy Blog article URLs return `410 Gone`, are absent from navigation and sitemaps, and are not redirected to unrelated pages.

Romanian and English are the Public Content Locales. Romanian is the default: `/` redirects to `/ro`, while stable `/ro/...` and `/en/...` routes and an explicit selector retain the visitor's choice. Deep links are never automatically redirected by browser language. A localized page exists only when that reviewed version exists; there is no silent cross-language fallback. Blog posts may be published in one locale independently after launch, but all launch-required content is bilingual. Booking Session locale remains owned by the Booking App.

Header navigation is the beesolo wordmark linking Home, then Pricing, Blog, and Help, followed by **Sign in** and primary **Start free trial** actions. The footer links Blog, Help, FAQ, Privacy, and Terms. Docs, Changelog, Teams, Operations, API, developer, and repository destinations never appear in public navigation. The current site-wide command-palette search is removed; clear navigation is sufficient at launch, and scoped Blog or Help search may return only when collection size justifies it.

Home uses the primary claim **Appointment scheduling for solo barbers and salon professionals** and positively explains one professional, one Shop, and one schedule. It contains: Hero; the Solo promise; a configure-publish-manage explanation; released capabilities for services and availability, public booking, appointments and series, customers, Waiting List and Walk-in Queue, notifications, and reporting; a customer-journey overview; Solo pricing preview; selected FAQ links; and a final trial action. It removes the fixture-backed Mara Booking Studio link and every production seed-Merchant example. It makes no unverified testimonial, customer-count, uptime, superiority, provider-logo, rating, review, or performance claim.

Pricing publishes only the €19 monthly or €190 annual Solo Plan and its fourteen-day trial, with current trial, billing, cancellation, recovery, and restriction facts. One non-interactive **Teams — coming later** note is permitted on Pricing only. It has no price, date, feature promise, waitlist, signup choice, navigation entry, account entitlement, upgrade path, or downgrade path. No other public copy may imply employees, staff accounts, multiple Providers, multiple Shops, collaboration, per-seat billing, or available Team behavior.

Blog remains public for merchant education and beesolo product updates. Every existing starter-architecture Blog post is deleted rather than renamed. Launch includes exactly two substantive posts in both Romanian and English: **Introducing beesolo: appointment scheduling for solo barbers and salon professionals** and **A practical online-booking checklist for a one-person Shop**. Later product updates belong in Blog rather than a version-number Changelog.

Help is the sole public home for product-use documentation. It launches with twelve bilingual checked-in MDX documents: start a trial and publish; configure Shop details, services, hours, and policies; share, preview, unpublish, and troubleshoot the public page; manage individual Appointments; manage Appointment Series; operate Walk-in Queue and Waiting List; manage Customer Records, notes, bans, exports, and Privacy Requests; understand email, WhatsApp/SMS routing, failures, and Messaging Balance; understand Solo trial, billing, restriction, cancellation, and recovery; customer booking/rescheduling/cancellation; customer Waiting List and Walk-in participation; and customer Privacy Requests. All twenty-four starter/developer Docs MDX files are deleted, not mixed into Help.

FAQ is pre-purchase content, not operational documentation. Its ten launch questions cover audience fit; Team support; Solo price and trial; Customer Accounts; online customer payment; supported notifications; multiple Shops or calendars; cancellation and billing failure; customer-data protection; and where merchants and customers get help. Answers state limitations plainly, send operational detail to Help, and give the Team teaser no commitment.

Blog and Help remain checked-in MDX with no launch CMS. Validated frontmatter records content type, locale, slug, title, description, publication and update dates, translation key, and draft state. Drafts do not produce routes, metadata, feeds, or sitemap entries. Builds reject duplicate localized slugs, missing launch-required Help translations, broken internal links, and forbidden starter vocabulary.

Privacy and Terms must contain real, reviewed beesolo legal copy before production: the operating legal entity and contacts, governing terms, billing/trial/cancellation rules, data practices, processors, retention, customer rights, and privacy-request route. Generic templates, fictional particulars, and “adapt this” placeholders may exist only outside production and fail release checks. **Define beesolo Privacy Request Operations** owns the intake and handling behavior; this decision owns the public route, discoverability, and legal-page links.

Every indexable localized page has a unique title and description using lowercase beesolo, a self-referencing canonical URL, `hreflang` for available Romanian and English translations, and `x-default` pointing to Romanian. Sitemaps contain only live localized pages, published Blog and Help documents, and effectively available Merchant Public Booking Pages. Published and ready Merchant pages are indexable by default with authoritative merchant metadata. Unpublished, unavailable, degraded, preview, Booking Session, checkout, continuation, and Confirmation surfaces are `noindex` and absent from sitemaps. Structured data uses only authoritative legal-operator, offer-price, and merchant facts and never invents translations, availability, identity, ratings, or reviews.

Production release checks crawl rendered Romanian and English routes, metadata, manifests, sitemaps, feeds, and assets for retired identity; allow the Team teaser only in its approved Pricing location; prove retired routes return `410`; prove private routes are excluded from indexing; prove Merchant-page indexing follows effective availability; validate canonicals, `hreflang`, `x-default`, localized links, locale retention, MDX schemas, required translations, and broken links; reject legal placeholders, fixture Merchants, fake proof, unsupported claims, and unapproved branding; verify desktop, mobile, keyboard, and screen-reader navigation and calls to action; prove no Team product path exists; prove active package, repository, and deployable-resource identity has migrated safely; and pass build, unit, route-ownership, accessibility, and Playwright regression suites.
