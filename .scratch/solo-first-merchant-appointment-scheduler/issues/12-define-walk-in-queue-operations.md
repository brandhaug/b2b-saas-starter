# Define Walk-in Queue Operations

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 05

## Question

What Shop configuration, enrollment, ordering, wait-time estimate, Owner-Provider assignment, Owner authorization, status transition, concurrency, customer-linking, notification, abandonment, and reporting rules turn the existing Walk-in Queue capability into a complete BeeSolo operating loop?

## Comments

### Resolution — 2026-07-31

BeeSolo's **Walk-in Queue** represents physically present demand at its one Shop. Remote interest belongs to the Waiting List. Customers may enter through self-enrollment or Owner-assisted enrollment, but self-enrollment requires a **Shop Presence Capability** obtained from the rotating onsite QR code or code displayed while enrollment is Open. The code rotates every fifteen minutes; a successful enrollment consumes the obtained capability, and opening, closing, or reopening immediately invalidates previously displayed codes. The permanent public walk-in page may expose Open, Closed, or Full state and an approximate new-entry wait, but it cannot admit a customer without presence proof. BeeSolo uses Turnstile, rate limits, and generic rejection responses rather than GPS or location permission.

The Owner explicitly opens **Walk-in Enrollment**; BeeSolo never auto-opens it. Opening is available only during a current effective working interval, with current subscription access and at least one walk-in-eligible Active Service. Enrollment automatically closes at the end of that working interval, when access no longer permits new demand, or when the Owner closes it. Closing affects only new admission: it never removes an active Walk-in Entry, and the Owner may finish the queue or deliberately start remaining customers outside current hours under the ordinary warning and override rules.

Walk-in Service eligibility is an explicit subset of Active Services and defaults to none. Enrollment selects exactly one eligible Primary Service and any eligible Additional Services permitted by the normal booking rules. The sole Owner-Provider is implicit; launch stores or exposes no Provider preference or assignment choice. Enrollment snapshots Service names, customer-facing durations, resolved buffers, and prices. Later catalog edits do not silently rewrite that request. While an entry is Waiting or Called, the Owner may confirm a revision with the physically present customer: Service revisions use current Active Service facts, Customer Details revisions supersede stale unsent destinations, and every revision preserves before-and-after history plus the original enrollment snapshot. The customer cannot edit through the status surface. Once Serving, queue facts are immutable and later changes use the linked Appointment's operations.

The queue has a configurable maximum of one through fifty Waiting-plus-Called entries, defaulting to ten; Serving does not consume capacity. Public self-enrollment fails neutrally as Full at the limit. Owner-assisted enrollment may exceed it only after an explicit warning and private override reason. Capacity allocation is atomic. BeeSolo warns when the projected wait extends beyond working hours but does not close automatically on that basis.

Enrollment requires a customer-facing name; email and phone are optional. It conservatively matches or creates and links a Merchant-scoped Customer Record in the same transaction under the existing exact-contact rules. Name alone never matches. The linked record owns the Walk-in activity and is reused when the entry becomes an Appointment. Customer Ban checks precede admission; Owner-assisted enrollment may use the existing explicit ban override and required reason. A uniquely matched Customer Record may have only one Waiting, Called, or Serving entry in the Shop. A repeat request from the same browser capability returns the existing protected status rather than creating another entry. Shared or ambiguous destinations do not block a distinct customer and instead follow the existing possible-duplicate behavior. Additional Services belong on the existing entry rather than a second entry.

New entries join the end of the Waiting order by FIFO enrollment time. Only Waiting entries have numbered positions. The Owner may reorder Waiting entries, but every move requires a short private reason and is preserved in entry history. Calling a later customer therefore requires reordering first. A **Called** entry leaves the numbered queue and owns the next feasible service opportunity without promising an immediate start; one Called entry may coexist with one Serving entry. At most one of each may exist. **Return to Waiting** releases the Called slot and restores that entry to position one by default; deliberately placing it lower uses the ordinary reasoned reorder. Called has no automatic response timeout.

The **Walk-in Wait Estimate** is derived rather than persisted. From the current instant it simulates the sole Owner-Provider's timeline using the planned remaining occupied duration of Serving, the Called entry, all earlier Waiting entries with their snapshotted durations and buffers, Scheduled Appointments as fixed interruptions, and Blocked Time or non-working intervals that lack a committed override. Customers see “about N minutes,” rounded up to five minutes, never an exact promised start. Enrollment, reorder, Service revision, lifecycle mutation, schedule mutation, and Appointment mutation recompute the view. Scheduled Appointments always take precedence because a queue position reserves no future time.

The lifecycle is Waiting, Called, Serving, Served, Removed, or Expired. Owner Call moves a Waiting head to Called. Return to Waiting moves Called back to the front. Customer **Leave Queue** may move only Waiting or Called to Removed with customer-left provenance. The Owner may remove Waiting or Called using Customer Left, No Response, Duplicate or Error, Shop Unable to Serve, or Other; Other requires a private note. Closing enrollment does not expire entries. At the end of the Shop-local calendar day, the Background Worker atomically moves unresolved Waiting and Called entries to Expired. Serving never expires or self-removes. Served, Removed, and Expired never reopen; another visit creates another Walk-in Entry.

**Start Walk-in Service** is the explicit Called-to-Serving boundary and atomically creates exactly one linked Scheduled Appointment at the actual start instant. It revalidates the current entry revision, active selected Services, queued snapshot, Customer Ban or override, subscription handling, scheduling facts, and conflicts. It may warn and require an override reason for working-time or Blocked Time conflict, but overlap with another non-terminal Appointment is forbidden. The Appointment links the already-selected Customer Record and preserves both the current queued snapshot and original Walk-in provenance. The queue status surface remains the customer access path, so creation does not emit a duplicate ordinary booking confirmation.

**Finish Walk-in Service** atomically moves Serving to Served, completes the linked Appointment, and applies the normal Pay In Person completion choice: record an External Collection, Already Recorded, or Collect Later. **Abort Walk-in Service** is the only supported path when started service cannot complete: it atomically moves Serving to Removed and cancels the linked Appointment under the ordinary cancellation, customer-message, and External Collection return rules while preserving actual start and abort facts. Generic Appointment cancellation must redirect to this coordinated command while a linked entry is Serving so the two lifecycles cannot diverge.

Only the authenticated Owner may configure, open, close, reorder, call, return, edit, start, finish, abort, or remove. An Impersonated Merchant Session receives only that effective Owner authority and preserves full System Operator provenance. Customers receive only Shop Presence enrollment, entry-scoped inspection, and Leave Queue. The Background Worker receives only end-of-day expiry authority. The protected **Walk-in Status Access** shows that customer's status, selected Services, position, and advisory estimate and exposes Leave Queue only while Waiting or Called. It never exposes other customer identities or requests. Access remains valid while active and for twenty-four hours after a terminal outcome. Owner-assisted enrollment may present, copy, or send its QR/link; Owner-assisted reissue after checking the present customer revokes earlier access, while email or phone alone cannot discover an active entry.

Every command carries an idempotency key and expected Walk-in Entry revision; ordering commands also carry the observed queue-order revision. Enrollment, capacity, active-customer uniqueness, the single Called slot, and the single Serving slot are enforced atomically. Start revalidates the complete Appointment conflict set and commits the Appointment, entry transition, history, Customer link, and notification work together. Finish and Abort commit their linked Appointment and entry effects together. Concurrent Leave, Call, Return, reorder, edit, Start, Finish, or Abort operations use first-commit-wins: a stale loser receives refreshed current state and changes nothing. Retries cannot duplicate entry history, notification intents, Customer links, External Collections, or Appointments.

Enrollment creates its ordinary confirmation Notification Intent with Walk-in Status Access whenever an eligible destination exists. Call creates the urgent “spot assigned” intent through every permitted operational channel. Return to Waiting after Call creates a correction intent. Owner removal notifies only for Shop Unable to Serve or Other; Customer Left, No Response, Duplicate or Error, Expired, Serving, and Served create no automatic queue message. Position and estimate changes update the protected view without outbound messages. Notification submission or delivery failure never rolls back a queue or Appointment command. [Define Transactional Notification Workflows](11-define-transactional-notification-workflows.md) owns exact routing, wording, retries, delivery, and Messaging Balance effects.

Closing enrollment, lowering capacity, or removing a Service from walk-in eligibility affects only future admission. Existing entries preserve position and snapshots. If a selected Service becomes Inactive, Start is blocked until the Owner revises to an Active Service or removes the entry. Schedule and Appointment changes update estimates without deleting demand. A newly matched Customer Ban requires the explicit override before Start. Restricted Access closes new enrollment, but an already admitted physically present customer is an existing commitment: the Owner may still call, return, start, finish, abort, or remove that entry under the ordinary rules.

Every entry and queue command preserves immutable actor, any impersonating System Operator, server time, prior and resulting revision, before-and-after facts, reason or override where required, linked Appointment operation, and Notification Intent identity. Reporting groups historical snapshots and events by Shop-local day, hour, and selected Service. It includes enrollment and terminal counts by reason, enrollment-to-Served conversion, enrollment-to-Start actual wait median and p90, estimate error against actual start, Start-to-Finish service-time median and p90, peak queue length, and maximum observed wait. Reorders, overrides, delivery outcomes, and outside-hours service remain auditable but are not headline metrics. Linked Appointment Price Snapshots and External Collections may appear as separate operational facts and are never represented as verified revenue. Later Customer or Service edits do not rewrite historical periods.
