# Merchant Messaging Settings

Own the provider-neutral Merchant projection and mutation for Appointment messaging
configuration. Keep the contract Merchant-scoped and limited to enablement, the four
launch purposes, the supported reminder lead time, delivery-window copy, safe runtime
state, and read-only controlled-template previews.

Do not expose provider names, route order, raw evidence, destination data, arbitrary
template editing, or financial operations through this capability. Actor applications
must establish Merchant Owner authority before invoking it.
