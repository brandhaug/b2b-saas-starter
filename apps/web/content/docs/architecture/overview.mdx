The Booking Product ships six Workers: the unauthenticated Public Site, the
authenticated Merchant App, the staff-only Operations App, the private Booking App,
the merchant-scoped Platform API, and the Background Worker. They share one D1 model
through Effect capabilities while Merchant and System Operator auth realms stay
separate.

Customer traffic enters at the Public Site and reaches the Booking App through a
service binding. The Booking App atomically commits the Appointment, Confirmation
access metadata, idempotent replay result, and durable outbox row before returning.
Queue publication is only a wake-up; the Background Worker also sweeps D1 every
five minutes, so provider outages do not undo a booking.

Merchant cookies stay on the Merchant App. Booking Session and Confirmation
credentials are exact-path, host-only cookies and only hashes persist. Platform API
tokens are single-Merchant, explicitly scoped bearer credentials.

See the repository's `ARCHITECTURE.md` for the full topology, privacy matrix, and
explicitly deferred first-slice behavior.
