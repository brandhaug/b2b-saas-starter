Confirmation appends a `booking_outbox` row in the same D1 transaction as the
Appointment. The Queue only wakes the Background Worker; a five-minute sweep
recovers work if publication fails. Email and every Webhook are attempted and
recorded independently, so delivery failure cannot roll back or hide an Appointment.

Webhook messages carry stable Merchant, Appointment, event, and trace identifiers,
not Customer Details or credentials. Requests are HMAC signed with the endpoint's
one-time-disclosed secret. Consumers should verify the timestamp/signature and
deduplicate on the stable event ID.

Failed or stale claims remain recoverable by a later queue invocation or scheduled
sweep. Operators inspect delivery history through the Merchant App or a token with
the Webhooks scope; they never recreate an Appointment to repair notification work.
