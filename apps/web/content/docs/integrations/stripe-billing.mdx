Billing is an env-gated feature with Stripe as its provider. Public pricing content renders without Stripe, and the starter validates `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` and `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` when they are present. Checkout, portal redirect, and webhook handler routes are planned but not yet implemented in `apps/{web,api,background}/src`.

## Env gate

The Stripe variables are optional fields in `packages/env/src/server.ts` (see the secret matrix in `ARCHITECTURE.md`). Leaving them unset keeps billing inactive — public pricing still renders, and subscribe buttons fall back to a "Configure billing" message.

## Webhook handling

Inbound Stripe webhooks will land on the background Worker. The route, signature verification, plan-state updates, and `auditEvents` writes are not yet implemented.

## Customer portal

A workspace customer-portal redirect is part of the planned billing surface. It lands together with workspace-role enforcement at the route layer when billing activates.

## What Stripe is not used for

Stripe is not used for user authentication, not for invitation emails, and not for storing PII beyond the customer ID and last-four card display. The starter's domain stays in D1.
