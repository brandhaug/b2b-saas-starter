Authentication is owned by the application whose identity it establishes. The unauthenticated Public Site has no auth factory, cookie, or catchall route.

## Merchant Auth

- Verified email/password access for Merchant Members
- Host-only `merchant` cookies and authoritative D1 sessions
- No stock Better Auth administration or raw impersonation endpoint

## Operations Auth

- A distinct secret, origin, trusted-origin list, and host-only `operations` cookies
- Mandatory TOTP and backup-code enrollment
- Four composable Better Auth roles with product-owned Operations permissions
- No public signup and no stock user/session administration surface

Operations authorization rechecks current D1 state on every protected request. Merchant membership never grants Operations authority.

## Impersonated Merchant Sessions

An approved System Operator crosses into the Merchant App only through a single-use, 60-second handoff submitted by top-level POST. The resulting one-hour session retains `impersonatedBy` provenance and reduced authority; it never merges with a normal Merchant Session.

## Optional customer identity

Customer identity uses its own cookie namespace and cannot grant Merchant or Operations authority. Anonymous booking remains complete when customer providers are unconfigured.
