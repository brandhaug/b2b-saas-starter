# Provision System Operators through controlled invitations

After the first operator is created by the bootstrap script, only a System Operator with `operator:manage` may invite another dedicated staff identity and assign its initial Operator Permissions. An Operator Invitation is single-use, expires after 24 hours, and grants nothing after expiry or revocation. Acceptance creates a 30-minute permissionless Operator Enrollment Session restricted to password setup, email verification, TOTP enrollment, backup-code confirmation, and sign-out; only completed enrollment yields an Operator Session. If enrollment expires after password setup, signing in creates another enrollment-only session without requiring a new invitation. Operators cannot change their own permissions, and the system rejects disabling, deleting, or removing `operator:manage` from the last enabled operator who holds it, preventing self-escalation and administrative lockout.
