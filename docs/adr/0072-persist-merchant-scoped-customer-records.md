# Persist Merchant-scoped Customer Records without Customer Accounts

beesolo persists a durable Customer Record inside each Merchant boundary instead of deriving the Customer Directory only from Appointment snapshots. This narrowly supersedes ADR 0054's deferral of a customer identity registry: the record is reusable Merchant-owned directory identity, not a verified Customer Account or cross-Merchant profile. Public and Merchant-created Appointment confirmation conservatively matches or creates the record in the Appointment transaction, while the Appointment retains immutable Customer Details snapshots.

The durable record is necessary for private Merchant Notes, preferred contact points, destination-specific consent evidence, bans, imports, deduplication, merges and split recovery, and enforceable retention. Exact normalized email or phone may attach only one unambiguous, non-conflicting match; a name never matches by itself, and the Owner alone confirms ordinary merge or split operations. A permissioned System Operator may perform only the narrower Privacy Identity Partition established by ADR 0073 when proven privacy scope must be separated from a mistaken merge without Merchant approval. This accepts possible duplicates rather than risking false identity consolidation.

Ordinary record edits never rewrite Appointment snapshots. Audited privacy erasure is the exceptional path that irreversibly anonymizes personal snapshot values while preserving non-personal Appointment facts. beesolo—not the Merchant Owner—verifies and executes public Privacy Requests under the standing Merchant instructions established by ADR 0073; no Customer Account is required, and no record or credential grants cross-Merchant access.
