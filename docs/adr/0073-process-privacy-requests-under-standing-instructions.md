# Process Privacy Requests under standing Merchant instructions

The Merchant remains controller for its Merchant-scoped Customer Records and Appointment customer data, while beesolo acts as processor under standing documented instructions in the Merchant's accepted Data Processing Agreement. Those instructions authorize beesolo to intake, verify, review, disclose, correct, partition mistaken merges, and erase data without case-by-case Merchant approval; beesolo is independently controller only for its own security, operator-access, abuse-prevention, and legally required audit data. This preserves the Merchant's controller role without making a customer's rights depend on the Merchant responding to an individual request.

Every disclosure or mutation requires an explicitly permissioned System Operator decision bound to an immutable preflight, while accountless exact-destination proof and risk detection may proceed automatically. Ambiguous merged identity may use a narrowly scoped Privacy Identity Partition rather than exposing or erasing the whole Merchant record. Erasure is resumable and irreversible: it quarantines identity and communication first, executes a fixed value-minimized manifest idempotently, respects independently authorized Retention Holds, and completes only after provider obligations and a post-erasure scan pass.

Because Cloudflare D1 Time Travel can restore data from before an erasure, a value-free Privacy Action Ledger lives outside the primary Merchant-data restore boundary. Any restore remains in maintenance mode until corrections, quarantines, erasures, and holds absent from the restore point are replayed and the privacy scan passes. This additional recovery authority is preferred to either disabling point-in-time recovery or accepting that an operational restore could silently resurrect erased personal data.
