# Use a single-use impersonation handoff

The Operations App and Merchant App preserve their separate origins and host-only cookies by transferring impersonation authority through an opaque Impersonation Handoff Ticket. After operator permission, reason, concurrency, and fresh-TOTP checks succeed, Operations persists only the ticket hash and audit metadata; the browser sends the plaintext ticket in a top-level cross-origin POST rather than a URL. The Merchant App atomically consumes the ticket, creates its host-only Impersonated Merchant Session, and redirects to the Merchant dashboard. A ticket expires after 60 seconds; expired, replayed, mismatched, or partially consumed tickets fail closed. If the receiving browser already has a normal Merchant App session, the Merchant App rejects the handoff instead of replacing, saving, or later restoring that identity. Manual stop, expiry, or authoritative revocation clears the Merchant impersonation cookie and returns the browser to the target Merchant Member detail in the Operations App with an outcome notice; the separate Operator Session remains unchanged.
