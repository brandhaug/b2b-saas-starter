# Notify Merchant Members of impersonation

The target Merchant Member receives email when impersonation starts and when it stops, expires, or is revoked. Notifications identify the Merchant, event time, optional external support reference, and security contact path, but do not disclose the internal Impersonation Reason or System Operator identity. The lifecycle transition and notification work commit together through the durable outbox; delivery failure does not block impersonation and is retried by the Background Worker. Target approval is not a prerequisite because recovery and incident-support cases may require access while the target is unavailable. This transparency makes platform access visible to the affected user without leaking internal personnel or case notes.
