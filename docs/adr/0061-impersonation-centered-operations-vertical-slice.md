# Start with an impersonation-centered Operations Vertical Slice

The first Operations App release includes operator authentication with mandatory 2FA, controlled provisioning, Merchant and Merchant Member search/detail, the complete permissioned impersonation lifecycle, and global impersonation audit review. Its permission catalog is limited to `merchant:read`, `merchant:impersonate`, `impersonation-audit:read`, and `operator:manage`; impersonation additionally requires read permission, and no wildcard or implicit super-admin permission exists. Merchant discovery searches Merchant ID, name, or slug and Merchant Member ID, name, or email. Merchant detail exposes identity, status, public-page status, readiness summary, and members; member detail exposes name, email, verification/enabled status, membership, active-session count, and last-sign-in metadata. Passwords, tokens, secret values, Customer Details, and full session tokens are never returned. Normal Merchant Member sessions cannot be revoked in this slice; that future security mutation requires its own permission, confirmation, reason, and audit policy. The slice does not recreate the legacy brand, Shop, customer, import, billing, loan, wage, or destructive-administration surfaces. Those capabilities carry different authority and risk, so they will be added later as independently permissioned Operations workflows rather than copied into the first slice.

Release-blocking automation covers auth-realm separation, mandatory TOTP and enrollment/recovery, single-session semantics, custom-role enforcement and stock-admin endpoint denial, identity disjointness, every handoff failure mode, impersonation concurrency/reduced authority/revocation/expiry, complete audit and durable notification behavior, and browser-level banner, stop, expiry, and return flows.
